Amaryll created the ravenous plant creatures that haunt the ruins of Cornath. A failed experiment meant to feed the hungry, the magically mutated plants gorge on the flesh of other creatures and require no sunlight to live. After plants kill a victim, most devour them from the inside out by taking root in the corpse. Fully satiated plants drop seed in the ground, which then grow into more murderous vegetation.

### Likes

Plant creatures like **beehives, the scent of morning dew,** and **the sound of rain**.

### Hates

Plant creatures hate **acid, ice,** and **clean shoes**.

#### \*Impale

If the plant gets a tier result on their branch attack, the target is grabbed by them. While grabbed this way, the target moves with if the plant is force moved and the target takes 1d6 P dam at the start of each of the plant’s turns. The plant can have any number of creatures grabbed this way.

#### \*Sliding Vines

If the plant gets a tier 2 result on a vine attack, they can slide the target up to 4 squares. If they get a tier 3 result, they can slide the target up to 8 squares.

#### \*\*Swallow

If the plant gets a tier 3 result on their bite attack, the target is swallowed by them. While swallowed, the target’s speed is 0, they take a bane on all tests, they take 1d10 dam at the start of each of the plant’s turns, and they don’t have line of effect to anything outside the plant and vice versa. If the plant dies, the target is no longer swallowed and appears in an unoccupied space of their choice within 1 square of the plant.
