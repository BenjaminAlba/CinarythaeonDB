# Laugh Eater

> Source: Crows — The Ref, Playtest 3, PDF page(s) 57. Original playtest wording retained.

A human almost split vertically in two by a bodysized mouth, the Laugh Eater was once a crow. Torn apart by demons nearly ten years ago, the crow’s laughed at the futility of life and existence as the shadows threw his limbs into a vortex of magic. The magic swirled around the bitter chortles, infusing them in the body it stitched back together.

The Laugh Eater’s spirit flits from dungeon to dungeon, metaphysically stalking crows who experience too much joy on the job. When the laughter of a crow crew becomes overwhelming within a spot watched by the eater, the being is made manifest, and comes to devour the joy and bones of those who summoned him.

#### \*Dismembering Bite

The eater scores a crit on a bite or a bite bite attack with a natural roll of 18, 19, or 20. If the eater gets a crit with their bite, their target loses a limb. Roll on the Dismember table in Characters to determine the limb lost.

#### Power Laugh (only once per turn)

As a maneuver, the eater unleashes laughter in a cube 3 within 1 square of them. They push each Large and smaller creature in the area up to 1d6 squares. A creature takes 1 dam for each square pushed.

#### Extra Action

The eater can take one additional action or maneuver on their combat turn.

#### Metaphysical Projection

The eater can project their consciousness into any dungeon to see what occurs within.

#### Metaphysical Travel (1/Rest)

As an action, the eater can teleport anywhere into a dungeon where its consciousness is.
