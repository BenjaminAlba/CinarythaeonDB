Beautiful and radiant yet twisted and corrupted, angels were once humans who fell into the hypnotic promises of Zaziel once the Archmage became the Great Light. Unlike many other monsters, angels seek light and shy away from the darkness. If you find yourself in the presence of an angel, their radiant beauty and harmonic sounds could distract from your impending death.

### Likes

Angels like **clean-burning fires as large as a bonfire or larger, the sound of beautiful, harmonized singing**, and **light sources with 3 or more UD in duration**.

### Hates

Angels hate **monsters who aren’t angels, thick smoke, fog, and other phenomena that blocks light,** and **spellbooks that don’t contain a necromancy or illusion spell**.

### Angel A

**Suggested names:** angel eye, light orb, spark

These angels look like floating spheres of light and can hide in plain sight within light sources. They can shrink as small as a coin and grow as large as a human skull. They latch onto victims and become brighter, empowering allies.

#### \*Attach

If an angel hits another creature with a burn attack, they can attach to the creature. While attached, the angel moves with the creature whenever they move, and the angel’s Inherit Radiance radius becomes 2/2.

#### Inherit Radiance

The angel sheds light 1/1. Any other angel in their bright light gains a +1 bonus to tests.

#### Hide in Plain Light

While within 1 square of an object that is also a light source, the angel is invisible.

#### Summon Angels

As an action, the angel summons a group of angels that appear in unoccupied spaces within 1 square of them. Roll 1d10. On a 1-5, 2 two angel A appear. On a 6-8, one angel A and one angel B appear. On 9-10, two angel A and two angel B appear. If this angel is hit with two tier 3 attacks in the same round, they can’t use this action until the end of the their next turn.

#### Join and Separate

As an action, the angel separates into two angels, angel H and angel I, who appear in spaces previously occupied by angel G. Angels H and I each have a Stamina total equal to half of angel G’s and any conditions or effects that were affecting angel G affect H and I. Those angels can each take a combat turn the round in which they appear. When an angel H and an angel I are within 1 square of each other, one of the angels can use an action to cause the two angels to merge into angel G. Angel G’s Stamina equals the sum total of angel H and I’s Stamina, and angel G suffers any conditions or effects that were affecting either angel H or I.

#### Radiance

The angel sheds light 5/5.
