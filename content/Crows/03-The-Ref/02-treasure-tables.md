When the PCs help travelers in need or scout the land for treasures and useful equipment as they travel, the rules call for you to roll one of the following tables. You can also roll on these tables when you want to give the PCs treasure or a reward, but aren’t sure what to give them.

Art objects, gems, lore books, spellbooks, ammunition, and weapons have tables you can roll on to determine their details. These tables get into as much detail as makes sense, but many still leave decision-making power in the hands of the Ref to prevent infinite rolling on tables.

## Weapons and Armor

If you need to generate a specific weapon because the Minor or Major Treasures table prompts you to do so, you can use the tables in this section to determine their specifics.

### Enchanted Items

If you want to create a random enchanted weapon or armor, first roll on the What’s the Enchanted Item table. From there roll on the appropriate table to determine the specific item. After that, roll on the appropriate materials table to determine what the item is made out of.

Once you have the base item, then roll on the Maximum Enchantment Complexity table. This determines the maximum total of all the item’s enchantment complexities.

Then roll for the item’s enchantments on the appropriate table. Keep rolling for enchantments until the total complexity of all the enchantments equals the maximum you rolled for on Maximum Enchantment Complexity table. If you roll for an enchantment that would make the item exceed the maximum complexity, ignore it and roll for a new enchantment.
