Entities of pure anger and hate, demons’ bright red eyes instill terror. Their shadowy forms contain the roiling rage of Ithnagio. Of all monsters, demons enjoy witnessing and inflicting pain the most. They can taste the fear of their victims, and the deeper it goes, the more delicious it becomes.

### Likes

Demons like **torture devices, desecrated religious iconography**, and **sounds of frustration and anger**.

### Hates

Demons hate **angels, light sources, displays of affection,** and **the smell of flowers and fresh- baked bread**.

## Demon A

**Suggested names:** black cloud, shade

These floating clouds of shadow sport glowing red eyes. They choke their enemies with vapors.

| --- | --- | --- |

#### Attacks

| Attack | Bonus | Range | 12–16 | 17+ |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Choke | +2 | Melee 1 | 2 P dam | 4 P dam |

#### Shadow Corruption

While the demon occupies an enemy’s space, the enemy takes a bane on all tests and takes 1 P dam at the start of each of their turns.

#### No Prone

The demon can’t be knocked prone.

#### Demonic Gaze

While the demon is in dim light or darkness, enemies within 3 squares of them take a bane on attacks.

**Suggested names:** emptiness, nothing, panic

| Hurl Dark | +2 | Ranged 10 | 3 dam | 7 dam |

#### Hole Pull

When the demon gets a tier 3 result with their tentacle attack, they can pull the target into themselves. The target is grabbed. While grabbed this way, the target takes 1d10 P dam at the start of each of the demon’s turns and can’t target any creatures or objects aside from the demon and they can’t be targeted by any creatures or objects. If the target uses the Escape Grab maneuver, they use Mind for the maneuvers test to escape this grab. The demon can have any number of creatures grabbed this way.

#### Move Under Prey

The demon can move into and occupy the space of any creature. When a creature starts their turn in the same space as the demon, they must make an Agility RR.

| RR result | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | The creature falls into the demon as if Hole Pull was used on them. |
| 2 | The target can shift 1 square and takes 2 P dam. |
| 3 | No effect |

#### Demonic Gaze

While the demon is in dim light or darkness, enemies within 3 squares of them take a bane on attacks.

## Demon H

**Suggested names:** dread cloud, nullification

A shadow of pure hate with no fixed shape, this demon can be inhaled by another creature and make a shadow copy of their body.

#### Statistics

| Size | Power | Type | Stamina | Speed | Reactions |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Holy Shit! | 26 | Demon | 130 | 7, fly 7 | 4 |

| Agility | Mind | Strength |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 4 | 5 | 2 |

#### Attacks

| Attack | Bonus | Range | 12–16 | 17+ |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Choke | +5 | Melee 1 | 10 P dam | 20 P dam |
| Hurl Dark | +5 | Ranged 10 | 10 dam | 18 dam |

#### Shadow Echo (only once per turn)

The demon uses an action, targeting a non-demon creature within 1 square of them who must make a Strength RR.

| RR result | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | The target is cloned. |
| 2 | The target takes 1d10 P dam. |
| 3 | No effect |

The clone is a demon with the same statistics as the target whose unarmed strikes deal 5 dam on a tier 2 result and 10 dam on a tier 3 result. The clone has the Shadow Echo trait. To permanently kill the demon, you need to kill the clouds and all clones.

#### Shadow Form

The demon can move through and occupy the space of any creatures, objects, or other obstructions. If an enemy starts their turn or enters the demon’s space for the first time on a turn, they must make a Strength RR.

| RR result | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | 10 P dam |
| 2 | 5 P dam |
| 3 | No effect |

#### Unkillable

If the demon dies, but clones they have created are still alive, then the demon reforms with all their Stamina in a place within 1 mile of their death chosen by the Ref.

#### Extra Action

The demon can take one additional action or maneuver on their combat turn.

#### No Prone

The demon can’t be knocked prone.

#### Demonic Gaze

While the demon is in dim light or darkness, enemies within 3 squares of them take a bane on attacks.
