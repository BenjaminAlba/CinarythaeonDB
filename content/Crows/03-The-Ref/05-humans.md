Humans are the other people of Cornath that interact with the PCs. Hirelings, bandits, cultists, and other NPCs use the human stat blocks. For more information on hirelings, see *Characters*.

Many humans the PCs interact with don’t need stat blocks, but if they do, you can choose the most appropriate one from below.

## Ending the Fight

As sapient creatures, most humans flee or surrender when they’re clearly outmatched. Even most humans with souls warped by the Miasma still have a logical part of their brain that says, “Get out of this and live to fight another day.”

Most humans have a reason for fighting another group. They are defending themselves, their loved ones, or their property, robbing the other person, or have some emotional desire to do harm (though perhaps not murder). Once that desired is fulfilled, they stop fighting. A human defending their home doesn’t pursue a fleeing threat, and a bandit robbing a passerby stops stabbing once they have a full purse.

A human may also stop fighting if someone or something they love is threatened, or if presented with a desirable quid pro quo.

When a human fighting alone drops to 0 Stamina, they flee, unless the Ref deems otherwise. When a group of humans battling the PCs has their number halved, they flee, unless the Ref deems otherwise.
