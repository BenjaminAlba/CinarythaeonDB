# Elemental of Regret

> Source: Crows — The Ref, Playtest 3, PDF page(s) 56. Original playtest wording retained.

The pure distillation of an emotion forged into a cosmic being by magic, few creatures compare in power to elementals. They stood at the start of time, and when magic stirs, they too become restless.

The Elemental of Regret harnesses the trauma of the world, bringing forth the memories of person’s worst moments. They can’t manifest any other emotion. Joy, rage, fear—these belong to other elementals. The Elemental of Regret exists to remind others of their failures and their worst moments. Without these memories, we cannot strive to do better. They are the guiding hand of shame that prevents the repeating of history.

This elemental manifests as an enormous constellation of constantly moving diamond teardrops. They rain regret down upon humanity, and if challenged in combat, reality around them bends to become more sorrowful.

#### Rain Sorrow (only once per turn)

The elemental uses an action to rain crystals in a cube 5 within 20 squares of them. Each creature in the area must make a Mind RR.

| Tier | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | The creature is vulnerable, weakened, and prone. |
| 2 | The creature is weakened. |
| 3 | No effect |

#### Strange Brain (3/Day)

The elemental uses a maneuver to move up to their speed, spreading the regret of strangers into the minds of their foes. Each creature whose space the elemental occupies during this time must make a Mind RR.

| Tier | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | The creature can’t wield any items it currently holds until the end of the DT. |
| 2 | The creature immediately dumps the contents of their backpack on the ground. |
| 3 | No effect |

#### Warp World (3/Day)

The elemental uses a **maneuver** to cause the world around them to become dull gray and sorrowful. **Each creature within 5 squares** of them must make a **Strength RR**.

| Tier | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | 10 P dam and the creature must use their lowest characteristic for all tests until the end of the DT. |
| 2 | 5 P dam |
| 3 | No effect |

#### Sorrow (only once per round)

When a creature within 20 squares damages the elemental, the elemental can use a reaction to deal P dam equal to half the amount the creature dealt to that creature.

#### Two Extra Actions

The elemental can take two additional action or maneuvers or one additional action and one maneuver on their combat turn.

#### No Prone

The elemental can’t be knocked prone.
