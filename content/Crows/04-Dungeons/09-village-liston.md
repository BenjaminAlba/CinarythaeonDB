# Village: Liston

> Source: Crows — Dungeons, Playtest 3, PDF page(s) 2. Original playtest wording retained.

The monks of Liston Abbey dug into the soft forest earth when the Necronmancer War began to hide from monsters and Miasma. They took in refugees and over decades their tunnels grew, creating a village in the earthen halls. It has the following institutions and their stewards:

- **3rd-Level Alchemist:** Cuthbert

- **1st-Level Auction House:** Edith

- **1st-Level Barracks:** Piers

- **1st-Level Blacksmith:** Alfred

- **2nd-Level Bookseller:** Gertrude

- **1st-Level Crow Guild House:** Jackdaw

- **1st-Level Enchanter:** Wulfric

- **2nd-Level General Store:** Winifred

- **3rd-Level Temple:** Dreddard

- **1st-Level Inn:** Ethel

- **2nd-Level Crypt:** Clara. Boons available: Flight (1), Knowledge (1), Swiftness (1), Vitality (2).
