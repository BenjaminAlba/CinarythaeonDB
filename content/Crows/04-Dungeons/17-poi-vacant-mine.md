# POI: Vacant Mine

> Source: Crows — Dungeons, Playtest 3, PDF page(s) 11. Original playtest wording retained.

When the PCs reach the mine, they find:

- Gray stone juts out around a metal frame where a heavy metal door hangs open.

- Painted on the door is a hasty note written in dark streaks: “It whispers. It whispers. Run.”

## Mineshaft

- A dark tunnel follows a slow slope downward. Lanterns that once lit the tunnel have long gone cold.

- The tunnel opens into a sprawling cavern that drops down 60 feet. Beautiful ores glitter along the far stone face below, and supplies left by miners line the wall.

- A creaking set of stairs switchback down the abrupt rock face.

- Hazy purple gas with an iridescent blue tint hangs around the ground floor of the cavern.

### Stairs

The stairs, which include six switchbacks, are long, crumbling, and have one gap every switchback. To leap the gaps without equipment, the PCs must make the following test. PCs who use equipment either gain an edge on their test or bypass the test (at the Ref’s discretion).

**2d10 + A**

| ≤11 | 12-16 | 17+ |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The structure collapses, and the PCs plummet to the next level of the stairs. Each PC takes 4 dam. | The PC leaps the gap, but the structure groans. The PC takes a bane on their next test to cross a gap. | The PC leaps across the gap. |

### Treasures Within

The mineshaft contains a vein of **necromancer silver**. A pile of it—enough for six bars—has already been mined and left out in the open.

The supplies left behind include **2 miner’s picks**, a **shovel**, **2 lanterns**, a **tent**, and **42 gc**.

If a PC uses a miner’s pick and a shovel, they can work the mine for more necromancer silver. One PC can mine 1 tons of ore in 8 hours of mining, and 1 ton of ore produces enough necromancer silver to make four bars (once crushed and smelted). Every 8 hours spent mining earns the PCs an encounter check with an EN of 4 as the sound echoes loudly through the mineshaft.

A ton of ore occupies 500 slots, and requires a vehicle to move.

### Gas

When the miners were excavating the necromancer silver, they struck a vent that unleashed arcane gas. A creature who spends more than 1 minute in the gas begins to hear whispers and see hallucinations of frightening, unearthly things. A creature who spends more than 10 minutes begins to feel paralysis in their limbs and feels that something is after them. After 30 minutes, a creature becomes unable to move until they finish a rest in clean air.

A creature can double the amount of time they can spend in the gas before they feel each stage of effects by wearing a mouth covering or making a **Strength RR**.

**2d10 + S**

| ≤11 | 12-16 | 17+ |
| --- | --- | --- |
| No effect | The PC can spend 1 hour in the gas before succumbing to paralysis. | The PC can spend 3 hours in the gas before succumbing to paralysis. |

The PCs can recognize the gas for what it is and stop the leak by locating the vent and plugging it with a successful Mind test.

**2d10 + M**

| ≤11 | 12-16 | 17+ |
| --- | --- | --- |
| No info | The PC knows that the gas is and locates the vent, but they halve the amount of time they have before they succumb to paralysis. | The PC knows what the gas is, locates the vent, and knows it can be plugged with clay or similar materials. |
