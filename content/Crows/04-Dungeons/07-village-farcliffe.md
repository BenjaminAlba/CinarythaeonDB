# Village: Farcliffe

> Source: Crows — Dungeons, Playtest 3, PDF page(s) 1. Original playtest wording retained.

The people of Farcliffe retreated into their silver mine when the Necromancer War began, taking everything they could, including their village’s name, with them. It has the following institutions and their stewards:

- **2nd-Level Alchemist:** Aalis

- **2nd-Level Barracks:** Raoul

- **1st-Level Blacksmith:** Mahaut

- **1st-Level Bookseller:** Melisende

- **1st-Level Crow Guild House:** Onfroi

- **2nd-Level Enchanter:** Aimery

- **1st-Level General Store:** Rollant

- **3rd-Level Stables:** Isabeau

- **1st-Level Temple:** Jehanne

- **3rd-Level Inn:** Garnier

- **1st-Level Crypt:** Bernadette. Boons available: Cooperation (1), Escape (1), Knowledge (1).
