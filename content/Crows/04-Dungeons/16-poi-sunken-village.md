# POI: Sunken Village

> Source: Crows — Dungeons, Playtest 3, PDF page(s) 9–10. Original playtest wording retained.

When the PCs reach the village, they find:

- The quaint wooden homes of this village succumb to the massive swamp, each one sunken into the mire as plants take root in their beams.

- Once known for its proximity to natural resources, Hampstead has a history of humble magical concoctions. Broken, faded, and fallen signs advertise an apothecary and a potion maker’s shop.

- In the central square, an enormous silhouette stirs and resettles. The air is thick and heavy with the scents of damp earth and burgeoning plants.

- While most first floors of the cottages are underwater, some houses have second floors above the waterline.

The swamp water makes Hampstead entirely difficult terrain (unless the PCs climb onto roofs or other structures).

## Village Square

- Murky water drowns the village square. Only the upper halves of abandoned market stalls can be seen, their canopies torn, faded, and damp.

- Fog hangs low around a plant creature rooted in the water here. Their many Venus flytrap heads slowly open and close gaping maws as vines slither through the swamp water, seeking a meal.

### Ravenous Plant

The ever-hungry **plant G** in the village square attacks and attempts to devour any creature who enters its reach.

### Market Stalls

Searching the drowned remains of the market stalls reveals 2d6 decorated pots (**Tiny art objects worth 30 gc each**) and 1d6 pieces of handmade jewelry (**Tiny art objects worth 50 gc each**).

## Apothecary

- This small shop, once welcoming with its potted and hanging plants, is now overwhelmed by rampant plant growth.

- Most shelves are underwater, their contents broken. The upper shelves and display cases hold dusty bottles.

### Hidden Flora

Three **plant B** and one **plant E** are camouflaged in the harmless plants in this room. They attack if the PCs search the shelves.

### Loot

The shelves hold **two healing potions**, one **greater healing potion**, and a **group healing spellbook**.

## Potion Maker’s Shop

- Strange colors shimmer over the swamp water in the potion shop like oil slick due to broken bottles and a tipped-over, mostly submerged cauldron.

- Two sickly trees with rotting, pustule-covered fruit sit in the corners by the back shelves.

- The few preserved bottles hold potions of various colors.

## Rotted Flora

Two **plant C** lurk in the corner of this room. As soon as a PC moves closer, they throw spikes to deter them.

### Loot

The shelves hold a **speed potion**, a **rage potion**, and a **phasing potion**.

## Cottages

- A dozen cottages sit, partially sunken, many tilted and starting to crumble.

- Most cottages have broken or open windows that lead to the second floor.

There are twelve searchable cottages—the rest are too sunken or dilapidated to hold anything of value. One or more PCs can search a cottage, but doing so earns them an encounter check with an EN of 8. If an encounter occurs, use the Plant Encounters table in *The Ref*.

If the **plant G** in the village square isn’t defeated, the PCs make an Agility test to move from cottage to cottage without alerting it. This plant G has feasted here long enough to grow vines that can cover the entire village—the PCs aren’t beyond its reach until they leave Hampstead.

**2d10 + A**

| ≤11 | 12-16 | 17+ |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The plant G is alerted and attacks with their Vine. | The PCs move to the next cottage without incident, but all take a bane on the next test made to sneak. | The PCs move to the next cottage without incident. |

Each cottage holds **2d10 gc** and something on the Minor Treasures table in *The Ref*.
