# POI: Cult Shrine

> Source: Crows — Dungeons, Playtest 3, PDF page(s) 2–3. Original playtest wording retained.

When the PCs reach the shrine, they find:

- Broken stone walls frame a crumbling, mossstrewn statue of a woman. Her face has been roughly chiseled at to turn half of it into a skull, and her hands are cupped in tender benefaction above an altar.

- Behind the shrine, black-robed cultists camp in scattered tents circled around a fire.

- Undead wander in halting steps through the camp, bloated beneath their well-tended clothes.

## Cult Encampment

- A half-dozen tents shelter supplies and ritual materials.

- Several cloaked cultists chant around a fire as they throw offerings to Ornassa into the flames.

- An undead draft horse is tethered near a cart of bundled supplies. Its dark, matted flesh bears wounds from a whip.

### Cultists and Undead

Six human cultists (four **cultists (power 4)** and two **priests (power 3)**) make offerings to Ornassa. Their undead loved ones (four **undead A**) stalk around the camp.

The cultists attack if a PC comes within their line of sight. The undead attack if the PCs make a loud noise. If one group attacks, the other group is alerted to their presence and joins in.

Sacks around the fire hold various supplies and ritual items: three **necromancer deathtree logs**, a **necromancer deathtree crossbow**, a **death ring**, and a **deadspeech spellbook**.

### Tents

One or more PCs can search the six tents, but if the cultists and undead are alive and nearby, searching a tent earns the PCs an encounter check with an EN of 5. If an encounter occurs, two cultists have a loud conversation as they walk toward the tent. If they find intruders, they shout and every human and undead in the camp investigates and attacks.

The PCs can find one item from the Minor Treasures table in *The Ref* per tent.

### Draft Horse

Fleabiscuit, the cultists’ **draft horse**, was recently reanimated after dying to a pack of wolves. He has no loyalty to the cultists, who profess loyalty to human connections but overwork their animals, and can become a pet if a PC makes the following test.

**2d10 + M**

| ≤11 | 12-16 | 17+ |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fleabiscuit is frightened and alerts the camp. If the PC and Fleabiscuit survive, the horse follows the PC at a distance for 48 hours before leaving. The PC can attempt to bond with him to become his owner (see Characters). | Fleabiscuit is wary of the PCs and doesn’t heed them right away. If the PCs free him from the camp, Fleabiscuit becomes friendly. | The PC becomes Fleabiscuit’s owner. |

The cart beside Fleabiscuit holds a **surgical kit**, **2 necromancer silver bars**, 4 black jades (**gems worth 10 gc each**), and **26 gc**.

## Shrine to Ornassa

- Signs of cult worship are laden across this statue of Ornassa. Her face has been roughly chiseled, turning half of it into a mask, and black jades and carved half-skulls have been offered at her feet.

- An altar holds a black stone offering bowl filled with gold coins. The floor before it is stained dark and smells of iron.

### Loot

Ten black jades (**gems worth 10 gc each**) are offered to Ornassa, as well as a few small carved half-skulls. The PCs recognize this as a symbol of Ornassa.

The offering bowl holds **148 gc**.

### Traps

The cultists have trapped the shrine to ensure no one steals their offerings. One trip wire is hidden 4 inches off the ground among the rubble near the door. It’s attached to a bell that alerts the encampment if triggered. A second, thinner trip wire is hidden two inches off the ground just behind the first. If triggered, an eruption and a **smoke bomb** go off, and each creature standing in the shrine takes 1d6 dam from the eruption as the camp is alerted.

A PC who examines the shrine before entering can make the following test to notice the trip wires.

**2d10 + M**

| ≤11 | 12-16 | 17+ |
| --- | --- | --- |
| No info | The PC notices only the first trip wire. | The PC notices both trip wires. |

If a creature enters the shrine without noticing the trip wires, the traps trigger and the encampment is alerted and hostile.
