# POI: Ruined Sawmill

> Source: Crows — Dungeons, Playtest 3, PDF page(s) 7. Original playtest wording retained.

When the PCs reach the sawmill, they find:

- A rotting wooden sawmill stands along a river, its wooden beams a beautiful framework of once-industry and decay.

- As the river surges around the wheel, the machinery room groans, its mechanisms and interior saws lurching and seeking logs that aren’t there.

- A broken wooden door shows an entrance into the sawing floor.

- A ramp leads from the river to the sawing floor, where rafts of logs were sent down the river to be processed. Several rafts are jammed together along the ramp.

## Sawing Floor

- Deep claw marks gouge the wooden door and its frame.

- The air is humid, thick with the smell of blood and flesh.

- The long carriage system is strewn with dust and wood chips. The sash saw’s rusted blades jerks up and down in sporadic intervals.

### Carriage System

Searching the sawing floor near the carriage system finds a **handaxe** and an **axe**. Four sawn yew logs lay along the carriageway. One sawn **archmage willow log** is hiding in the pile.

### Crows (of Multiple Kinds)

If the PCs come halfway into the room, they see one **giant crow** and four **crows** picking at the corpse of a crow (the human kind) in the corner near the machinery room. The human crow was scavenging supplies from the mill, not knowing this flock nested in the small attic space above where the machinery sits. These crows are territorial and attack if they notice the PCs within the sawmill.

If combat begins, the giant crow moves any creature it has grabbed toward the sash saw. If a creature is pushed into the saw, roll a d6. On a result of 1 or 2, the river is surging and the sash saw is violently sawing. The PC takes 1d6 dam from the blade if it’s unmoving and 3d6 dam from the blade if it’s moving.

## Machinery Room

- The heavy machinery strains the moldering floor as belts, wheels, and other mechanism strain to keep this abandoned system working.

- Worker’s bags are scattered around the room.

### Collapsing Machinery

The floorboards have rotted due to the humidity and water over the years. If a Medium or larger creature tries to traverse this room, they must make the following Agility RR. If two or more Medium or larger creatures search the room at the same time, they each take a bane on their test.

**2d10 + A**

| ≤11 | 12-16 | 17+ |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The machinery collapses. Belts snap, large machinery lurches, and the floor falls out into the river. Each PC in the room takes 10 dam, and the supplies are lost. | The machinery partially collapses. Belts snap and some parts of it fall. Each PC in the room takes 5 dam. | The floor and machinery remain intact. |

### Abandoned Supplies

This room holds a **lantern**, **blacksmith’s tools**, **50 feet of rope**, and **42 gc**. At your discretion, the room can also hold a map to buried treasure of your choice within 2 hexes of the mill.

## Ramp

- Along the ramp lie several rafts carrying decaying logs and old satchels.

- Both the ramp and the rafts dip partially below the water, slowly decomposing.

Searching the rafts and satchels reveals **two hickory logs**, **two rations** (and a third that is molding), a **coin purse** holding **25 gc**, and a faded note that says, “Money for new sluice gate. Praise the Smith.”
