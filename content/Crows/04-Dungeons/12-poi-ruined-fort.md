# POI: Ruined Fort

> Source: Crows — Dungeons, Playtest 3, PDF page(s) 5–6. Original playtest wording retained.

When the PCs reach the fort, they find:

- The rolling grassy plains give way to a looming stone fort that bears the wounds of battle and time.

- The walls surrounding the courtyard are partially crumbled, scarred, and blackened by eruptive magic.

- Bandits patrol the ramparts, and the flickering red orange of fire against the interior courtyard walls indicates more bandits within.

Along the back are towers that hold barracks and officer’s quarters and are accessible only from inside the fort. The other side, which holds the stable, has completely collapsed.

## Courtyard

- Two bandits patrol the ramparts, looking out at the grassy plains in slow, confident strides, knowing their vantage point is absolute.

- A group of bandits eat and drink within, their gaunt faces sharp with hunger and greed. They talk rarely, their former brotherhood hollowed out by the Miasma.

### Bandits

These bandits are Miasma-touched. The two patrolling bandits are **archers (power 4)**. The five bandits inside are two **thieves (power 3)**, a **trapper**, a **pike warrior (power 5)**, and an **illusionist (power 2)**. Their leader, a **thief (power 9)** is in the officer’s quarters. If combat beings in the courtyard, their leader hides, approaches stealthily, and attacks from a distance before joining combat.

If the combat continues for two more rounds and the bandits have not one, the bandit leader calls for two others from the officer’s quarters: an **alchemist** and a **priest**.

### Spoils

In addition to their equipment, the bandits have looted **212 gc** and two turquoise (**gems worth 75 gc each**) off crows they’ve killed.

## Barracks

The door to the barracks is locked with a padlock, which can be unlocked with a key held by the trapper in the courtyard. A PC can attempt to unlock the padlock with the following test.

**2d10 + A**

| ≤11 | 12-16 | 17+ |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The padlock remains locked and the person inside the barracks shouts “Who’s there?” alerting the bandits in the officer’s quarters. | The padlock is unlocked, but clicks loudly, and the person inside shouts, “Who’s there?” alerting the bandits in the officer’s quarters. | The padlock is unlocked. |

Inside are the barracks and Rekhya, a crow.

- Among the beds and weapon racks sits a woman in leather armor, her hands bound. She has a black eye and signs of rough handling.

- Around her, the weapon racks hold rusted weapons from before the fort’s ruin.

### Rekhya

This dark-skinned, black-haired woman knows the location of a treasure stash nearby, and the bandits are trying to torture it out of her. She offers to split the stash with the PCs if they free her. If they do, she also fights with them using a sword from the barracks. She is a **sword warrior (power 4)**.

If the PCs agree to her offer, she takes them to a treasure stash when they’re finished at Fort Summer. It contains **2d100 gc** and five items from the Major Treasures table. If the PCs try to take more than 75% of the gold coins and four items, Rekhya becomes hostile.

### Arms of War

Most of the weapons are worthless, but if the PCs search the weapon racks, they find some weapons in good condition: a **halberd**, a **glaive**, two **maces**, and a **sword** that Rekhya takes if freed.

They can also find two sets of **light armor** and one set of **medium armor** that aren’t decayed beyond use.

## Officer’s Quarters

- The door to the officer’s quarters is open. The sleeping quarters are sparse, looted for everything worthwhile.

- (*If the bandits didn’t leave this area to attack*) A large bandit with two wicked knives plays cards with two other bandits, one with vials along her belt and the other with a spellbook on his lap.

The bandits have largely emptied this area.

The bandit captain carries **two knives with the hungry property**, a **blood hide light armor**, and **52 gc**.
