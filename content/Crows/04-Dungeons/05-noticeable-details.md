# Noticeable Details

> Source: Crows — Dungeons, Playtest 3, PDF page(s) 1. Original playtest wording retained.

Every area of a POI or dungeon starts with a bulleted list of details the PCs notice when they enter for the first time, provided they can discern any details in darkness. You can read these bullet points aloud or paraphrase them as you see fit.
