# Village: Burrfield

> Source: Crows — Dungeons, Playtest 3, PDF page(s) 1. Original playtest wording retained.

The fortified crypt of Queen Burrany was dug into the ground and seal centuries ago. Desperate to escape the perils of the Necromancer War, refugees from other village broke into this sacred place and founded their village. It has the following institutions and their stewards:

- **1st-Level Alchemist:** Ada

- **3rd-Level Barracks:** Hildegard

- **2nd-Level Blacksmith:** Otto

- **1st-Level Bookseller:** Adalbert

- **2nd-Level Crow Guild House:** Orvis

- **3rd-Level Enchanter:** Wilhelm

- **1st-Level General Store:** Gisela

- **1st-Level Stables:** Segimer

- **1st-Level Temple:** Dietrich

- **1st-Level Inn:** Mathilde

- **1st-Level Crypt:** Gertrude. Boons available: Cooperation (1), Disappearance (1), Fury (1).
