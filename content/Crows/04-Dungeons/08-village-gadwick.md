# Village: Gadwick

> Source: Crows — Dungeons, Playtest 3, PDF page(s) 2. Original playtest wording retained.

Gadwick is contained in the towers and expansive dungeon and crypts of the once-grand Castle Gadwick. It has the following institutions and their stewards:

- **1st-Level Alchemist:** Brune

- **1st-Level Auction House:** Lili

- **2nd-Level Barracks:** Cormal

- **2nd-Level Blacksmith:** Deirdre

- **1st-Level Bookseller:** Rion

- **1st-Level Enchanter:** Isaac

- **3rd-Level General Store:** Sorcha

- **1st-Level Stables:** Anna

- **1st-Level Temple:** Mackle

- **1st-Level Inn:** Duna

- **1st-Level Crypt:** Oda. Boons available: Greed (1), Rescue (1), Vitality (1).
