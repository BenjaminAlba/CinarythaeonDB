# Crows Source Differences

28 items have distinct printed wording or values. The main quick reference prioritizes the General card PDF, then Professions, then Dungeons. This is an editorial presentation choice, not an official errata ruling. Each affected note retains the alternate card text and its printed header/value. Some differences are shortened descriptions or omitted quality upgrades rather than contradictions.

Notable checks before play: **Chain** price; **Holy Symbol** stack; **Smoke Bomb** price/crafting fragment; **Whistle of Dog Summoning** price; **Wound Closure Book** rank; **Minor Healing Book**, **Minor Ward Book**, and **Minor Blessing Book** targets/effects.
