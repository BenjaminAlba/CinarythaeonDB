## Card rules

This book relates to a lore expertise or Alchemy, Blacksmithing, or Enchanting. Study this book as a rest activity and you gain that expertise and 1 additional Effort that you can use only with that expertise. This benefit lasts until you finish a new rest.

Fine (1,000 gc): Gain 2 additional Effort.

Masterwork (2,000 gc): Gain 3 additional Effort.
