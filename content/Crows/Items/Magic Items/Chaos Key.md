## Card rules

UD 1 (Rest; Activate)

**Maneuver:** You place this key in a closed door with a lock that has a slot for a key and it fits. When the door is opened, it leads to a random location determined by the Ref in the same building or dungeon. The door leads to this location until the key is removed from the lock.
