## Card rules

While you wear both of these gloves, you can touch a door with them as a maneuver. The gloves then speak aloud, at a normal conversational volume, any significant creatures or objects within 5 squares and on the other side of the door that can easily be seen by a creature who can see in the dark who stands in the doorway.
