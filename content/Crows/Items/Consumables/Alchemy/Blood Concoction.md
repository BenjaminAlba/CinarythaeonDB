## Card rules

**Maneuver:** You consume the potion and must make a Strength RR. You can remove this from a belt slot and consume it in one maneuver.

| Result | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| ≤11 | Your lower body is now a slug tail. Your speed is reduced by 2. |
| 12-16 | You gain both the tier 1 and 3 results. |
| 17+ | Your eyes are compound and you can see in the dark. |

Unique Item | Worth: 500 gc
