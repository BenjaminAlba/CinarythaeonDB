## Card rules

**Maneuver:** Consume to gain the ability to move through walls and other objects for 2d6 combat rounds. If you stop moving inside of an object, you take 1d10 P dam and are shunted out into the space where you entered it. You can remove this from a belt slot and consume it in one maneuver.
