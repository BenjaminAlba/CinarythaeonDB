## Card rules

**Maneuver:** Refuel lantern.

**Maneuver:** Spread over a 2 x 2 area of ground. A Creature entering must make an Agility RR.

| Result | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| ≤11 | Prone |
| 12-16 | Lose 2 squares movement this turn |
| 17+ | No effect |

The area is flammable. If lit, it burns for 1d6 combat rounds. A creature who starts their turn in or enters the fire for the first time on a turn takes 1d6 dam.
