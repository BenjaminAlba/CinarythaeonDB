## Card rules

UD 1 (Rest; See Below)

**Action:** Hurl this box up to 5 squares and stone spiders crawl from it onto the creature closest to the box. The Ref chooses if creatures are equidistant. If a new creature moves closer to the box, the spiders swarm them instead. At the end of a combat round, the creature takes 1d6 dam, and roll this item’s UD. If all the spiders or box get wet or no creature is within 5 squares of the box at the end of a round, the box loses its UD.
