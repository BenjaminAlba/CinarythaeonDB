## Card rules

Illusion Man.

UD 1 (Rest; Activate)

Ranged 10 Dur. 1 UD

You create a phantom image that you direct to make noise and give off smell for the duration within a space within range. You can use a maneuver to make the image move up to 5 squares. The maximum size of the image is based on the casting.
