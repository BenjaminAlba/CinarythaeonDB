## Card rules

Illusion Man.

UD 1 (Rest; Activate)

Self Dur. 1 UD

You become invisible and generate a number of illusory doubles according to the casting within 5 squares of you. These doubles look and sound like you. You can make them speak independently, and you can use a maneuver to have each move 5 squares. If a double takes damage, it disappears. When all doubles disappear, you become visible.
