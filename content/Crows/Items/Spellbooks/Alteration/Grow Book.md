## Card rules

Alteration Man.

UD 1 (Rest; Activate)

Melee 1 Target 1 creat.

Dur. 1 UD

The target and their equipment size are increased by a number of categories and weapon attack damage gains a bonus based on the casting. While growing, the target can occupy the space of other creatures and takes 1d10 P dam if they grow in a space too confined for their new size.

| Result | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| ≤11 | 1 size/0 reach/2 dam |
| 12-16 | 2 size/1 reach/4 dam |
| 17+ | 3 size/1 reach/6 dam |
