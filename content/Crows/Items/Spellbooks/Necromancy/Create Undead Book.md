## Card rules

Necromancy Act.

UD 1 (Rest; Activate)

Range 5 Target 1 corpse

Dur. 2 UD

You turn the corpse into an undead of your choice who acts as your pet. When the spell ends, the monster remains and becomes hostile to you. The monster’s maximum power is based on the casting and their size must be equal to the corpse.
