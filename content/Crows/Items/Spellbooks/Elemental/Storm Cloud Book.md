## Card rules

Elemental Act.

UD 1 (Rest; Activate)

Ranged 5 Dur. 1 UD

You create a storm cloud within range. Enemies in the cloud take 1d6+M dam at the end of your turn. As a maneuver, you can move the cloud up to 1d6 squares. The cloud is a cube with a size that depends on the casting.
