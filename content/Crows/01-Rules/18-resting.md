# Resting

> Source: Crows — Rules Booklet for Playtest 3, PDF page(s) 15–16.

You can rest to regain your Stamina, lose wounds, and recharge some equipment, like spellbooks. To rest, you must spend at least 6 uninterrupted hours in-game in one place without engaging in strenuous activity, such as exploration or combat, and at least 4 of those hours must be spent sleeping. While you rest, you must consume at least 1 ration or equivalent food. At the end of a rest, you regain all your Stamina and the number of wounds you have decreases by 1. You choose which wound you lose. If you rest outside of the Miasma (see Miasma), you regain Effort up to your maximum.

Rests take place outside of DTs. When you start a rest, your current DT ends, but the Ref doesn’t make an encounter check for the end of the DT. When you are halfway through your rest, any ongoing effects that end at the end of a DT or have UD that are rolled at the end of a DT also end. A new DT starts when your rest ends (which could be because you finish it or it could be because it is interrupted by a rest encounter).

If you don’t rest, you gain exhaustion wounds (see Exhaustion). If you finish a rest, you can’t benefit from another rest that begins until at least 6 hours or 3 DTs have passed.

## Rest Encounters

When you rest in a dungeon or out in the wilderness, the Ref should make an encounter check. If you rest in a dungeon, the Ref should use the encounter check rules for Dungeon

Encounters. If you rest during overland travel, the Ref should use the rules for Travel Encounters. If rest is interrupt by combat or other strenuous activity, the PCs must start resting again from the beginning to gain the benefits of a rest.

## Rest Activities

When you are not sleeping during a rest, you can engage in a light activity (usually done at the same time as being on watch while others sleep). The following rest activities are available to all PCs. Traits and other game rules grant can grant you additional rest activities. You can only perform on rest activity per rest and must complete the rest to gain the benefit of it.

### Craft Equipment

You can make one crafting roll to craft an item. See Crafting for more information.

### Good Night’s Rest

You get a sleep instead of doing any other rest activity. You gain 1 additional Effort that lasts until you use it or you finish a rest. You gain this additional Effort even if you rest outdoors in the Miasma.

### Harvest

You harvest crafting material from a monster corpse. The process destroys the corpse, and the corpse generates 1d6 parts if it is Medium or smaller, 2d6 parts if it is large, 3d6 parts if it is Huge, and 4d6 parts if it is Holy Shit.

### Identify Item

Choose one item you have that is magic. You experiment with the item and learn its properties when you finish the rest.

### Repair Armor

You repair a suit of armor or shield you have, restoring its AD to full when you finish the rest. The armor you carry, including the slots it takes up, includes a small kit for making basic repairs.

### Seclude Camp

You obscure the area where you and your allies rest, placing your belongings in accessible hiding spots, building ways to obscure your group. This activity lowers the EN during your rest by 1. Only one person in a group can perform this rest activity per rest and you don’t need to complete this rest to gain this activity’s benefit.

### Tend Wounds

Pick a creature who has at least 2 wounds who rests with you. You can’t choose yourself. You spend time tending to that person’s wounds. When you and that person finish the rest, they lose 2 wounds instead of 1. A creature can only benefit from this activity once per rest.

### Rest Activities Without Rest

You may want to accomplish several rest activities in a row, maybe to craft a potion faster or identify a bunch of new magic items you found. Outside of resting, a rest activity typically takes about 2 hours of uninterrupted time and a PC can perform a maximum of 4 per day. If such projects are undertaken during a situation tracked in DTs, the activities don’t resolve until the end of a DT (which the Ref may elect to end early in real-time for the sake of expedience). Any activities accomplished this way that have effects that kick in when you finish a rest instead kick in after the 2 hours spent working on them, with one exception—the Tend Wounds activity. A creature can only benefit from the Tend Wounds activity once per day, and they only gain the benefit after sleeping for at least 4 hours after the activity is completed.
