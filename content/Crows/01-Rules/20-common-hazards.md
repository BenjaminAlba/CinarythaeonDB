# Common Hazards

> Source: Crows — Rules Booklet for Playtest 3, PDF page(s) 17.

While all manner of hazards await the crows, these are some of the most common.

## Falling

When you fall 10 feet or more, you take 1d6 P damage per 10 feet fallen. You can use your reaction to make an RR to reduce the damage.

**2d10 + A**

**Tier 1:** No effect.

**Tier 2:** The damage is reduced by 1d6.

**Tier 3:** The damage is reduced by 2d6.

## Starvation

Each day a you go without eating at least 1 ration worth of food, you gain a starvation wound. A starvation wound functions like a normal wound, except that you remove all starvation wounds when you eat 1 ration or equivalent food.

## Exhaustion

Each day a you go without completing a rest, you gain an exhaustion wound. An exhaustion starvation wound functions like a normal wound, except that you remove all exhaustion wounds when you finish a rest.

## Suffocation

If you can’t breathe, the Ref tracks your time and movement in combat rounds. You can hold your breath a number of combat rounds equal to 3 + your Strength. At the start of each round after this that you can’t breathe, you take 1d6 P dam.
