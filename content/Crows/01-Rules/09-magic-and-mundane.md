# Magic and Mundane

> Source: Crows — Rules Booklet for Playtest 3, PDF page(s) 6.

The word “magic” describes creatures, objects, and effects that are magical in nature. The word “mundane” describes creatures, objects, and effects that aren’t magic. If an item is magic, it’s name appears in *italics*.
