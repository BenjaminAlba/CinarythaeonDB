# Crafting

> Source: Crows — Rules Booklet for Playtest 3, PDF page(s) 36.

While you rest or are in town, you can use to craft new equipment.

## Crafting Prerequisites

You must meet an item’s prerequisites to craft it.

- **Expertise:** Every craftable item has an expertise that you must have in order to craft the item.

- **Complexity:** Every craftable item has a complexity, which is the Mind score required to craft the item.

- **Materials:** Every craftable item requires raw materials. This prerequisite is on the item’s card.

- **Tools:** See below.

### Tools

The tools you need to craft an item are based on the expertise needed to craft the item.

- **Alchemy:** Alchemist’s tools

- **Blacksmithing:** Blacksmith’s tools

- **Enchanting:** Enchanter’s tools

### Harvesting Materials

Some crafting materials must be harvested from a monster corpse with the Harvest rest activity. The parts are monster organs and vials of blood collected from a corpse.

## Crafting Goal

Each craftable item has a crafting goal, a special number, listed on its card. The higher the number, the longer it takes to make the item.

## Crafting Rolls

You can take the Craft Equipment rest activity to craft a piece of equipment you meet the prerequisites for. When you do, you make a crafting roll when you finish the rest.

A crafting roll is a special Mind test and doesn’t have tiered outcomes. Instead, you calculate the total and record the result. When you make a crafting roll, the total—no matter how low— becomes crafting points that are accrued by the item you’re creating. Even if you have a bane or penalty on a crafting roll, the minimum total for the roll is 1, unless you get a doom result. If you get a doom result on a crafting roll, you accrue no new points for the item. If you obtain a crit on a crafting roll, you can make another crafting roll for the same item as part of the same rest activity.

When you apply a double edge or Effort to a crafting roll, the roll gains a +4 bonus instead of the normal benefits. When a double bane applies to a crafting roll, the roll gains a -4 penalty.

When you end the crafting rest activity, record the number of crafting points you accrued toward creating the item. Add these points to any previously accrued toward the item.

When an item first accrues points but isn’t complete, the materials needed to craft it are consumed and it is an unfinished craftable item, which has an inventory card.

When all of an item’s crafting points equal or are greater than its crafting goal, the item is complete and the materials used to create it are expended.

### Crafting Multiple Items

If you roll high enough to finish crafting an item, you can use any surplus points to begin crafting another version of the same item, provided you have the materials. If you have enough surplus points to craft a second item, then you can begin crafting a third, and so on.

### Multiple Crafters

If an creature taking a rest with you meets the tool and expertise prerequisites for an item you are crafting with the Craft Equipment activity, they can use their rest activity to undertake the task with you. Crafting rolls they make as part of this rest activity are accrued by the item as points.
