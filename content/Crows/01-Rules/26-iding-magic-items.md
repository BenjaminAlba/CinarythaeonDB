# IDing Magic Items

> Source: Crows — Rules Booklet for Playtest 3, PDF page(s) 37.

When you encounter an unfamiliar magic item, you don’t know automatically know its properties, but you can tell that it is magical, as all such items vibrate with power. To discover an item’s properties, you can use the Identify Item rest activity during a rest. You could also attempt to activate the item and see what happens if you don’t want to wait for your next rest to do so. If the item is a liquid in a vial, you might drink it or pour it out on the ground to observe any effect. If it’s a sword, you might swing it at a foe and see what happens. This method could waste a consumable item or have disastrous effects if the item is cursed or not fit for human consumption.

If you want to identify a magic item you hold, you can also make the following test.

**2d10 + M**

**Tier 1:** You accidentally activate the item in a harmful way determined by the Ref.

**Tier 2:** You learn nothing about the item.

**Tier 3:** You learn all of the item’s properties.

You can only make this test once with an item.
