# Characteristics

> Source: Crows — Rules Booklet for Playtest 3, PDF page(s) 6.

Every creature in the game, including each PC, has three characteristics:

- **Agility (A):** Agility represents a creature’s coordination and nimbleness. It’s used for dodging, attacking with ranged and light weapons, acrobatics, sneaking, and thievery.

- **Mind (M):** Mind represents a creature’s mental acuity. It’s used to resist mind-bending magic, cast spells, notice hidden or secret things, or recall lore.

- **Strength (S):** Strength represents might and brawn. It’s used for resisting poison and forceful effects, making melee attacks, climbing, jumping, lifting, swimming, and throwing.

Each characteristic has a score between -5 and 5. The higher your score, the better you are at the things that characteristic represents. PCs start with a score between -1 and 2 in each of these characteristics. The highest score a PC can have in a characteristics without magic help is 4.

### No Interaction Characteristic

You may have played some games where there have been rules that govern and statistics for social interaction between PCs and NPCs. Social interaction isn’t a focus of this game—many of an crow’s foes can’t or won’t be reasonable. We didn’t want to create a lot of social interaction expertises and traits that PCs would take and not use. Social interaction can be played out with the PCs and NPCs talking normally. The Ref decides if any persuading, bribes, lies, threats, or other techniques work on based on the NPCs’ personality. If the Ref wants the dice to decide if a PC outwits an NPC, they can use a Mind test.
