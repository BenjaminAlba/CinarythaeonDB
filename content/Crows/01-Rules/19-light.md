# Light

> Source: Crows — Rules Booklet for Playtest 3, PDF page(s) 16.

Without a proper light source, a crow can’t effectively navigate their environment, unlike dungeon denizens. There are three levels of light. When an item or effect sheds light, it’s listed as X/Y. X is the number of squares it sheds bright light in all directions, and Y is the amount of dim light it sheds in all directions after that.

## Bright Light

Bright light illuminates everything around it clearly. It’s sunlight and light that emanates directly from and closest to a source like a torch.

## Dim Light

Dim light poorly illuminates an environment, shrouding details in shadow, like moonlight. While attacking a target in dim light or making a test to discern details or search for something or someone, the test takes a bane.

## Darkness

While attacking a target in darkness or making a test to discern details or search for something or someone, the test takes a double bane. If you are attacking a creature who is moving silently, you must guess which space the target occupies. If you’re wrong, your attack automatically misses.

## Campfire

When the PCs rest (or whenever they feel like it), they can build a campfire. The light a campfire sheds depends on its size, as shown in the Campfire Brightness table. At the Ref’s discretion, building a Medium or larger fire takes significant time and requires a lot of wood.

**Campfire Sizes**

| Size | Bright | Dim |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tiny | 0 | 5 |
| Small | 5 | 5 |
| Medium | 10 | 10 |
| Large | 15 | 15 |
| Huge | 20 | 20 |

## Dousing Light Sources

If you douse a light source that has its duration tracked in UD rolled at the end of a DT, like a torch or a lantern, roll the UD when the light is doused as well.
