# Dungeon Encounters

> Source: Crows — Rules Booklet for Playtest 3, PDF page(s) 14–15.

The Ref should make an encounter check at the end of each DT and whenever the PCs do something within a dungeon that would attract a lot of attention, like making a lot of noise while breaking down a door with a hammer or setting fire to a huge tapestry and creating smelly smoke.

When the Ref makes an encounter check, they roll 1d10. If the number is equal to or lower than the encounter number (EN), then an encounter occurs (even if the PCs are in the middle of combat). The default EN for a check is 3, but the Ref can raise the number to 4 if a dungeon’s level is particularly crowded with creatures (more than twenty as a guideline) or if the PCs have left an inordinate amount of chaos and disorder (such as a trail of bodies) behind them. If both of these conditions are true, increase the EN to 5.

If an encounter occurs, the Ref should roll on the encounter tables in *The Ref Book* to determine what the PCs encounter. The table chosen should be based on the type of monsters that infest the dungeon. If more than one type of monster is in the dungeon, the Ref chooses a table. If the Ref rolled a 1 for the encounter check, the encounter occurs immediately. On a 2 or higher, the Ref immediately grants players some sign of the coming encounter, such as a monster heard roaring in the distant halls, a fresh and half-eaten corpse, or a large footprint, and the encounter occurs at any point during the next DT of the Ref’s choosing.

No rules or effects can change the EN to a number lower than 1.
