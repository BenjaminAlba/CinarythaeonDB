# Equipment

> Source: Crows — Character Booklet, Playtest 3, PDF page(s) 31–33.

An adventurer’s equipment is essential to their survival. Torches, swords, spellbooks, rations, and more make the difference between a big payday and your last day ever.

## Inventory Cards

Items that you can hold in your inventory are detailed on cards that are included with this game. These cards contain the item’s game statistics as shown on this page.

### Marking Cards

There isn’t a card included for every variation of an item within the game. For example, alchemist’s tools and masterwork alchemist’s tool use the same card, as do an iron sword and a steel sword. If you’re using a digital version of the game, you can copy and modify cards or print them out and write on them as you like.

If you have a physical copy of this game [*Playtest note: no one does yet*], clear plastic card sleeves were included with it. An inventory card fits inside of a sleeve and you can write on the sleeve with dry erase marker. All physical versions include the digital files, so you can also print out more cards as needed.

### Blank Cards

Blank cards are included with the game to allow the Ref to create their own equipment as needed.

### Think Outside the Card

Inventory cards detail specific ways you can use an item, but that’s not all you can do with that equipment. You can do anything with it that you could do with in real life. Just because a sword’s card doesn’t say anything about it being used as an impromptu pry bar, doesn’t mean you can’t use it that way. You can turn some 11-foot poles and a tent into a raft! The sword might break, and the raft might sink, but isn’t all that gold worth giving it a shot?

## Money

The currency of Cornath is gold coins (gc). An inventory slot can hold 250 loose coins or 1 purse that holds up to 500 gc.

The folks of Cornath still value gold, because a common currency is the best and most efficient way to trade between villages. Though the Necromancer War ravaged the land, society still exists in the walls of villages, and the hope of an eventual return to normalcy has only grown since the end of the conflict.

## Shopping

The equipment the PCs can buy depends on the level of the merchants in their village. See Village for more information.

## Gear

Gear is a term that encompasses all the mundane equipment crows use that doesn’t include armor or weapons. A compass, a grappling hook, and even a torch—it’s all gear.

The Gear table shows the cost of each item. If an item can be crafted, the table shows its crafting expertise, complexity, materials, and goal.

Gear can be purchased at a general store institution. If an item requires the blacksmithing expertise, it can also be purchased at a blacksmith. If it requires an alchemy expertise, it can be purchased at the alchemist.

### Fine and Masterwork

Some pieces of gear have a fine and masterwork version indicated on their card, each being an improved version of the standard. The full price of the fine and masterwork versions are listed in parentheses next to the description of their improved statistics. Other than price, the fine and masterwork statistics stack with the item’s standard statistics, but not with each other.
