# Alchemical Items

> Source: Crows — Character Booklet, Playtest 3, PDF page(s) 34.

Acids, bombs, poisons, potions and more are crafted through a process called alchemy. Sometimes alchemy is pure science. Sometimes it’s science and a magic.

The Alchemical Items table shows the cost of each item and its crafting complexity, materials, and goal. Alchemy is the expertise used in crafting these items. Alchemical items that are magic are *italicized*. The items on the table can be purchased from the alchemist institution.
