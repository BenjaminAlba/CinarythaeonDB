# Crow Creation

> Source: Crows — Character Booklet, Playtest 3, PDF page(s) 1.

To create a crow, follow these steps.

## 1. Roll For Background

You weren’t always a crow. Your background informs the statistics and equipment your bring with you when you first enter the profession. To get a background, roll 2d6 on the Backgrounds table. The first die result is the left d6 column of the table and the second is the right d6 column.

## 2. Record Statistics

Record your background’s statistics on a piece of paper with a pencil. Each background gives you some expertises, a Stamina score, and a starting trait from a trait tree (a full description of each trait is found in Traits). All PCs have a starting speed of 5 and maximum Effort of 2.

### Characteristic Adjustments

You background makes one of your characteristics a 2. Sometimes the background assigns this increase. Other times it gives you a choice. Your remaining characteristics can be your choice of a 1 and a 0 or a -1 and a 2, assigned however your like.

### Name and Feature

Give your PC a name and a distinguishing feature. This could be a tattoo, distinct body odor, unique voice, strong personality trait, or the like. Just give them one thing stand out.

## 3. Equipment Cards

Your background also gives you a set of starting equipment. **Every PC has an empty coin purse, a knife, a rope, six rations, and 3d6 gc**.

## 4. Village Stuff

Make an NPC connection who lives in your village. If this is your group’s first time making PCs for a campaign, work with other players to make a village and name it (see The Village).
