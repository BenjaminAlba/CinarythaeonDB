# Enchanted Items

> Source: Crows — Character Booklet, Playtest 3, PDF page(s) 35.

Enchanted items are things that were once mundane that are then turned magical through a process of capturing magic in gems and embedding them into the item. Shafts of wood and metal are turned into wands. Clothing and jewelry become powerful pieces that grant more than fashion to the wearer..

The material component required to craft many enchanted items is gems equal to or greater than 20% of the item’s cost. Enchanted weapons and armor have their own material requirements, described in Armor and Weapons.

The Enchanted Items table shows the cost of each item and its crafting complexity, materials, and goal. Enchanting is the expertise used in crafting these items. All enchanted items are magic. The items on the table can be purchased at the enchanter institution.
