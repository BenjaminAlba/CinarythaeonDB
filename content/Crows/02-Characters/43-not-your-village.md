# Not Your Village

> Source: Crows — Character Booklet, Playtest 3, PDF page(s) 62.

When you travel to a village that isn’t your home, the Ref determines the village’s Prosperity and available institutions. The Ref doesn’t need to track cycles or events for these villages. They can decide what is there based on whatever makes the most sense for the story. More advice on making these villages is in *The Ref*.

### Sales in Other Villages

When you shop for items in a village other than your own, the prices of items you buy are increased by 20% of the base price. The shops in your village reward you with a discount for investing in them (or for tempting you into investing in them if you haven’t yet). This discount is baked into the price of each product, because the game assumes you’ll do most of the shopping at your home village.

Likewise, the sale price of items you sell in villages other than your own is 20% of the base price. You make in your own village because institutions in your village want to incentive you to keep investing in them.
