# Institutions

> Source: Crows — Character Booklet, Playtest 3, PDF page(s) 53–61.

Institutions are the merchants, temples, and other sites, services, and organizations that you can interact with while you are in the town.

Crows can spend their hard-earned treasure to found and level-up different institutions in town. They aren’t the only people in the village to invest in these institutions, so they don’t own them, but their contributions bring more money into the town and allow them to purchase more goods and services. The default price for equipment actually includes an investors discount, and stuff purchased at other villages has a higher price (see Other Villages).

You should write down and keep track of each institution’s level and it’s steward, which is the person who owns or is otherwise in charge of it. Stewards are NPCs played by the Ref. When you found an institution in your town, the Ref can provide the name and personality of the steward, or they can ask the player whose character invests in the institution to create the steward.

Each institution has the following details:

- **Founding Price:** The price to establish the institution in your village. When you pay to establish an institution, it doesn’t start operating until the start of the next village cycle.

- **Services:** The services provided by the institution are detailed here. Some services are only available when an institution achieves a specific level.

- **Level:** Each institution has a level that increases when you pay to advance it. An institution’s starting level is 1. Its maximum level is detailed on its advancement table.

- **Advancement Table:** An institution’s advancement table shows the price to increase an institution’s level, and the benefits that come from advancing the institution. When you pay to upgrade an institution, it doesn’t start operating at its new level until the start of the next village cycle. Merchant item availability is shown on this table.

### Alchemist

**Founding Price:** 500 gc

**Artisan** The alchemist can craft alchemy items that they have are given materials for. They make crafting rolls with a bonus equal to their level.

**Merchant** The alchemist provides the following services:

- **Alchemy Items:** They buy and sell any items that require the Alchemy expertise to craft. The alchemist only sells items of a specific complexity or less according to the institution’s level as show on the Alchemist Advancement table.

- **Alchemist’s Tools:** They buy and sell standard, fine, and masterwork alchemist’s tools.

- **Crafting Materials:** They buy monster parts that are used as crafting materials, but they don’t sell them.

- **Identify Alchemy:** They can provide the properties of any alchemy item brought to them for a fee of 10 gc per item.

- **Alchemy Workshop:** For a price of 5 gc a day, you can rent space in the alchemist’s workshop. For the day you do this, you have access to their facilities, which grant you a bonus on alchemy crafting rolls equal to the alchemist’s level.

- **Potent Poisons:** When the alchemist is 2nd or higher level and you buy a potion or poison from them with a duration listed in UD, you can pay 25% more for the item to have the duration increased by 1 UD.

- **Big Bombs:** When the alchemist is 3rd or higher level and you buy a bomb from them, you can pay 25% more for the item to have the size of its area of effect increase by 1.

- **Loyalty Health Potions:** When the alchemist is 4th level and the village’s Prosperity is 10, the first time during a cycle that you buy something from the alchemist, they give you a free *healing potion*.

**Advancement Table** The Alchemist Advancement table shows the availability of alchemy items based on the complexity required to craft them and the price increase the alchemist’s level.

**Alchemist Advancement**

| 2nd | 1,000 gc | 2 | Potent Poisons |
| 3rd | 2,000 gc | 3 | Big Bombs |

### Auction House

**Founding Price:** 250 gc

**Merchant** The auction house has the following services:

- **Buy Any Item:** Unless an item is truly unique or the rules state it can’t be purchased (like an elemental part), there is a chance it will be sold at the auction house. To figure out the availability of an item, tell your Ref what item you wish to buy at the auction house and then roll a d100 on the consult the institution’s advancement table. If the roll equals or is less than the auction house’s availability, then the item is for sale there during this cycle. Otherwise, the item isn’t available this cycle. If the item is mundane and available, the auction house has 1d10 such items in stock. If the item is magic, it has 1 such item in stock. You can reroll the availability of items at the end of a cycle. To figure out the cost of an available item, roll any die. If the result is even, the item is discounted by 1d6 × 10%. If the result is odd, the item costs 1d6 × 10% more.

- **Sell Anything:** You can sell any item at the house. Instead of the normal price, the item sells for 1d10 × (10 + village Prosperity)% of its value.

- **High Roller Auction:** When the auction house is 5th level and the village’s Prosperity is 10, once per cycle when you sell an item at the auction house and the item would sell for less than 80% of its sale price, you get 80% of the sale price instead.

**Advancement Table** The Auction House Advancement table shows the availability of items based on if they are a valued item or a unique item.

**Auction House Advancement**

| Lvl | Price | Availability |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1st | - | 20% |
| 2nd | 500 gc | 30% |
| 3rd | 1,000 gc | 40% |
| 4th | 2,000 gc | 50% |
| 5th | 4,000 gc | 60% |

### Beacon

**Founding Price:** 1,000 gc

**The Fire** The beacon is a tower that juts out of the village enclosure and is lit with an enormous flame at its top. This flame is tended to night and day, always burning with a special fuel that disperses nearby Miasma.

When the beacon is constructed, the hex containing the village and every adjacent hex is no longer affected by the Miasma. Humans in these hexes are unaffected by the Miasma, even while outdoors. The radius of the beacon’s effect expands every time the institution levels up.

**Merchant** The blacksmith provides the following services:

- **Fade Out:** You can pay to have yourself and up to four other creatures enter the flame of the beacon, and emerge in a hex affected by the beacon. The cost of this transportation is the number of hexes traveled × 100 gc.

- **Fade In:** You can purchase a *beacon medallion* for 500 gc. When you break the token while in a hex affected by the beacon from which it was purchased, you and up to four other willing creatures you choose within 10 squares of you are transported to the beacon.

**Burn Bright** When the beacon’s level is 5 and the village’s Prosperity is 10, the fire’s effective radius is 6 hexes.

**Advancement Table** The Beacon Advancement table shows the effective distance of the beacon’s fire and the price to increase the beacon’s level.

**Beacon Advancement**

| 2nd | 2,000 gc | 2 hexes |
| 3rd | 4,000 gc | 3 hexes |
| 4th | 8,000 gc | 4 hexes |
| 5th | 16,000 gc | 5 hexes |

### Blacksmith

**Founding Price:** 500 gc

**Artisan** The blacksmith can craft blacksmithing items that they are given materials for. They make crafting rolls with a bonus equal to their level.

**Merchant** The blacksmith provides the following services:

- **Blacksmithing Items:** They buy and sell any items that require the Blacksmithing expertise to craft, including barding. The blacksmith only sells items of a specific complexity or less according to the institution’s level as show on the Blacksmith Advancement table.

- **Blacksmith’s Tools:** They buy and sell standard, fine, and masterwork blacksmith’s tools.

- **Crafting Materials:** They buy logs and metal bars that are used as crafting materials, but they don’t sell them.

- **Armor Repair:** They can repair any armor you have for 5 gc per suit or shield repaired.

- **Blacksmith Workshop:** For a price of 5 gc a day, you can rent space in the blacksmith’s workshop. For the day you do this, you have access to their facilities, including the forge, which grant you a bonus on blacksmithing crafting rolls equal to the blacksmith’s level.

- **Extra Quality I:** When the blacksmith is 2nd or higher level and you buy a weapon from them, you can pay 25% more and the weapon gains your choice of the Brutal or Pummeling quality if it doesn’t already have it.

- **Extra Quality II:** When the blacksmith is 3rd or higher level and you buy a weapon from them, you can pay 25% more and the weapon gains your choice of the Defensive or Dismembering quality if it doesn’t already have it.

- **Hone Weapon:** When the blacksmith is 4th level and the village’s Prosperity is 10, they can hone a non-unarmed weapon for 500 gc. The weapon gains a +1 bonus to damage which doesn’t stack with additional honing from any blacksmith. If you get a doom on an attack with this weapon, it is no longer honed.

**Advancement Table** The Blacksmith Advancement table shows the availability of blacksmithing items and magic weapons and armor based on the complexity required to craft them and the price increase the blacksmith’s level.

**Blacksmith Advancement**

| 2nd | 1,000 gc | 2 | Extra Quality I |
| 3rd | 2,000 gc | 3 | Extra Quality II |

### Crow Guild House

**Founding Price:** 125 gc

**Free Services** The guild house has the following services available to crows:

- **Crows Stay Free:** Crows from any village are welcome to stay at the guild house.

- **Item Swap:** You can trade a magic item you have for an item of equal or lesser value with another crow. The maximum value of the item you can trade equals 1,000 × the institution’s level. The institution can make a number trades this way each cycle equal to its level.

- **Monster Catalogue:** You can record the names and descriptions of monsters you encounter in the guild house’s records. For every 5 monsters you catalogue, the guild house provides you with something rolled on the Minor Treasures table in *The Ref*. For every 20 monster you catalogue, they provide you with a roll on the Major Treasures table.

- **Shared Information:** You can ask a crow who has been to a POI or dungeon you know about any question about it. The crow answers the question honestly, though at the Ref’s discretion they may not know the answer (in which case you can ask a different question). You can get a number of answers equal to the institution’s level each cycle.

- **What’s in the Hex:** Choose a hex on the map. A crow staying at the guild house provides you with the location of any POIs, dungeons, or other villages you don’t know about within 1 hex of the chosen hex. You can access this service a number of times equal the institution’s level each cycle.

**Advancement Table** The Guild House Advancement table shows the availability of items based on their quality and cost to increase the store’s level.

### Crypt

**Founding Price:** 125 gc

**Honor the Fallen** When a crow dies during an adventure, their remains (whatever that may be), can be brought to the crypt and interred there. When the crow is interred, their player picks a boon from the list below. Once per cycle, the dead crow can instill upon another crow who prays at their grave gains the boon. A crow can only benefit from one boon at a time, and only one crow can have that dead crow’s boon at a time. If you already have a boon and pray for another, the new one replaces the old one.

Expending a boon requires no action. After you expend the boon, you no longer have it.

- **Boon of Cooperation:** When you make an assist, you can expend this boon to grant an additional bonus to the test you aid equal to twice the crypt’s level.

- **Boon of Disappearance:** You can use this boon to become invisible for a number of combat rounds equal to the crypt’s level.

- **Boon of Escape:** You can expend this boon to teleport a number of squares equal to three times the crypt’s level.

- **Boon of Flight:** You can use this boon to gain a flying speed equal to your speed for a number of combat rounds equal to the crypt’s level.

- **Boon of Fury:** When you deal damage with an attack, you can expend this boon to deal additional damage to the target. You deal an additional d6 damage to the target for each level the crypt has.

- **Boon of Greed:** You can expend this boon when you are in a dungeon to learn the direction of the most valuable treasures on the same level as the dungeon as you and how many chambers, including hallways, you must pass through to reach them. The number of treasures you learn about is equal to the crypt’s level, in order from most valuable to least.

- **Boon of Knowledge:** When you expend this boon, choose a specific creature, place, event, or organization with a proper name that you know. You can ask the Ref up to a number of questions equal to the crypt’s level about the subject, which they must answer honestly.

- **Boon of Rescue:** After you roll a RR, you can use this boon to improve the result by 1 tier.

You can use this boon a number of times equal to the crypt’s level before it is expended.

- **Boon of Swiftness:** When you expend this boon, your speed increases by a number equal to the crypt’s level until the end of the DT.

- **Boon of Vitality:** When you regain Stamina, you can expend this boon to regain additional Stamina equal to twice the crypt’s level.

When the crypt is 5th level and the village’s Prosperity is 10, it is considered 6th level for boon effects.

**Advancement Table** The Crypt Advancement table shows the cost to increase the crypt’s level.

**Crypt Advancement**

| Lvl | Price |
| --- | --- |
| 1st | - |
| 2nd | 250 gc |
| 3rd | 500 gc |
| 4th | 1,000 gc |
| 5th | 2,000 gc |

### Enchanter

**Founding Price:** 500 gc

**Artisan** The enchanter can craft enchanting items and enchant weapons and armor with any enchantments that they are given the materials for. They make crafting rolls with a bonus equal to their level.

**Merchant** The enchanter provides the following services:

- **Enchanting Items:** They can buy and sell any items that require the Enchanting expertise to craft or weapons and armor with enchantments on them. The enchanter only sells items and enchantments of a specific complexity or less according to the institution’s level as show on the Enchanter Advancement table.

- **Enchanter’s Tools:** They buy and sell standard, fine, and masterwork enchanter’s tools.

- **Crafting Materials:** They buy gems and creature parts that are used as crafting materials, but they don’t sell them.

- **Enchanting Workshop:** For a price of 5 gc a day, you can rent space in the enchanter’s workshop. For the day you do this, you have access to their facilities, which grant you a bonus on enchanting crafting rolls equal to the enchanter’s level.

- **Retrofit:** When the enchanter is 2nd or higher level and you buy a wearable magic item from them, you can pay 10% more to have the item turned into a belt, ring, hat, mask, necklace, or a pair of gloves or shoes instead of its normal form.

- **Last Longer:** When the enchanter is 3rd or higher level and you buy a magic item or enchantment that can become ineffective permanently or temporarily after its UD run out, you can pay 50% more to have the item gain 1 additional UD.

- **Ward:** When the enchanter is 4th level and the village’s Prosperity is 10, they can give a creature a personal ward for 500 gc. The creature gains 10 AD that lasts until removed by damage. This ward doesn’t stack with additional wards from any enchanter.

**Advancement Table** The Enchanter Advancement table shows the availability of alchemy items based on the complexity required to craft them and the price increase the enchanter’s level.

### Inn

**Founding Price:** 125 gc

**Merchant** You have a home in your village, so you don’t need to stay the night at the inn. However, if you’re in another town (or want to stay at the inn in your village for some reason), the cost of a night at the inn, which includes a day’s worth of food, is 5 gc.

**Gambling** As a rest activity, you can gamble at the common room of the inn. The maximum bet you can place is based on the inn’s level. Minimum bet is 1 gc. You can play the following games at the inn.

- **Over/Under 7:** State whether you think the result of a 2d6 roll will be over, under, or exactly 7 and make a bet. Then roll the dice. If you were wrong, you lose your bet. If you were right and guessed over or under 7, your money is doubled. If you guessed a result of exactly 7 right, your money is quadrupled.

- **Guess the Number:** You must guess a number from 1 to 6. Then the Ref rolls a d6. If you guessed the result incorrectly, you lose your bet. If you guessed correctly, your winnings are five times your bet.

**Find It Here** When the inn is 5th level and the village’s Prosperity is 10, you can find a special traveling merchant at the inn. Once per cycle, you can purchase any item that has a price from this merchant, but the cost of the item is increased by 25%.

**Advancement Table** The Inn Advancement table shows the maximum bet you can make at the inn while gambling and the price to increase the inn’s level.

### Temple

**Founding Price:** 250 gc

**Artisan** The priests of the Smith can craft alchemy, blacksmithing, and enchanting items and enchant weapons and armor with any enchantments that they are given the materials for. They make crafting rolls with a bonus equal to the temple’s level.

**Merchant** The temple has the following services:

- **Blessed Armor:** You can donate 200 gc or an item of equivalent or greater value to the temple and have the priests of the Warrior place a blessing on your armor. The armor’s AD increases by 2 × the temple’s level. This AD lasts until it is removed by damage. A suit of armor or shield can only benefit from one of these blessings at a time.

- **Greed Exchange:** You can place a magic item into a *chest of the Three*, an enchanted iron box, and close the lid and then reopen it. When you do, the item you placed inside disappears and is replaced with a new item. If the item is less than 100 gc, nothing happens when it is placed in the box. If the item placed inside is worth 100 to 500 gc, it is replaced with an item rolled randomly on the Minor Treasures table. If the item placed inside is worth 501 gc or more, it is replaced with an item rolled randomly on the Major Treasures table. Both of these tables are found in *The Ref*, and the Ref should make the roll, adding the temple’s level and the village’s Prosperity to determine the result. An item gained through the *chest of Three* can’t be used in another exchange with the chest.

- **Heal Wounds:** You can donate 100 gc or an item of equivalent or greater value to the temple and get a number of wounds equal to the temple’s level removed by priests of the healer.

- **Mount’s Prayer:** You can donate 100 gc or an item of equivalent or greater value to the temple and have the priests of the Gardner place a blessing on one pet that lasts for a number of days equal to the temple’s level. While this blessing is active, the animal’s speed increases by 3.

- **Prayer of Returning:** You can ask the priests of the Three to perform a ritual that returns an item you lost and is currently not in the possession of any other creature. The item must be one that you had in your possession for at least 24 hours and last had no more than a number of days equal to 5 times the temple’s level. The cost of retrieving the item is 50% of its value. For unique items that don’t have a value, the cost is 1d10 × 1,000 gc or an item of equivalent or greater value.

**Higher Authority** When the temple is 5th level and the village’s Prosperity is 10, it is considered 6th level for its services.

**Advancement Table** The Temple Advancement table shows the cost to increase the temple’s level.
