# Treasure

> Source: Crows — Character Booklet, Playtest 3, PDF page(s) 45–46.

Mundane treasure that isn’t coins is divided into two categories: art objects (jewelry, paintings, sculptures, etc.) and gems (diamonds, emeralds, rubies, etc.). Both have generic cards in the set that allow you to write the item’s specific name and value. Art object come in different sizes, and the number of slots they take up is show on the art object sizes table. The Ref determines the value of a gem or art object.

**Art Object Sizes**

| Size | Slots | Stack |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tiny | 1 | 5 |
| Small | 1 | 1 |
| Medium | 2 | 1 |
| Large | 16 | 1 |
| Huge | 54 | 1 |
