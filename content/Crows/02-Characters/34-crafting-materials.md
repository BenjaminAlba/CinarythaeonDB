# Crafting Materials

> Source: Crows — Character Booklet, Playtest 3, PDF page(s) 45.

Crafting materials (such as iron bars and demon parts) have generic inventory cards. Most of the time crafting materials are found on adventures. They can be sold to specific merchants, but most merchants don’t sell them, since they need the materials for their creations. A baker doesn’t sell flour, eggs, butter, or the other raw ingredients of their trade, and a blacksmith doesn’t sell iron bars. However, an auction house sometimes carries such materials.

The Crafting Materials table shows the base prices of crafting materials. Gems, which are used in crafting many enchanting items, can be worth almost any amount.
