# Vehicles

> Source: Crows — Character Booklet, Playtest 3, PDF page(s) 47.

Vehicles can be loaded up with treasure and supplies to make excursions to distant dungeons more survivable and worth the trek. Carts, wagons, and the like require animals to move across the land, while boats move under the power of humans, currents, and wind.

The vehicles tables shows each vehicle’s price, Stamina, size, and slots for how much equipment it can hold. Vehicles can also hold passengers, provided they aren’t loaded down with cargo. Each vehicle requires 1 human driving it.

The Land Vehicles table also shows the number of Large animals required to move it. A Huge creature counts as two Large animals for the purposes of moving a vehicle and a Holy Shit! creature counts as eight. Animals hailing a land vehicle can’t serve as mounts or have any of their slots occupied by equipment. All of their slots are considered filled by the vehicle they pull. A land vehicle’s speed and pace is that of the slowest the slowest animal moving it.

The Water Vehicle’s table shows the speed of each vehicle.

If a vehicle’s Stamina is reduced to 0, it is destroyed. A wheelwright (see The Village) can repair a vehicle.
