# Retirement

> Source: Crows — Character Booklet, Playtest 3, PDF page(s) 61–62.

When your PC has 60,000 or more TXP, you can retire them to the village. If you do, your PC becomes an NPC and no longer delves into dungeons. You can give your equipment to a worthy successor, perhaps a new PC you create or one of the other crows your retired adventurer delved alongside. A retired PC gives up the dangerous life of dungeon delving.

When you retire to the town, choose one of the following benefits that the town gains. If you have 100,000 or more TXP, you can choose two benefits:

- **Art Collector:** Your retired PC collects gems and art objects. Each cycle, they are willing to buy 1d6 art objects or gems from the PCs for 100% of their value.

- **Best Steward Ever:** Your retired PC becomes the steward of an institution in the village (taking over from the previous steward). This institution always operates as if the village’s Prosperity is 10, even if it is lower.

- **Crafty Connecting:** Your retired PC is connected to a merchant who deals in crafting materials. Each cycle, the PCs can purchase up to 1d6 crafting materials of any variety from them.

- **Crow Daddy:** Your retired PC is sure to give helpful tips to new crows. Choose one starting trait your retired PC knows. When you or another player creates a new PC from the village, that PC can start with that trait in addition to any other traits they start with. A new PC with 0 TXP can start with a maximum of three traits.

- **Generous Benefactor:** Your retired PC still loves to hear stories about the crow life and hangs out at the inn common room or a tavern listening to all the latest adventures. Once per cycle, the PCs can visit your retired PC who gives them a gift rolled on the Minor Treasures table in *The Ref*.

- **Master Mentor:** Choose up to three trait your retired PC knows. Other PCs can learn these traits from your retired PC, making them cost half as much XP as they normally do.

- **Old Crow Medicine Show:** Your retired PC learns all there is to know about medicine, poultices, and bandages. They can heal all wounds a PC or their ally has 1d6 times per cycle.

- **Reverend Old Crow:** Your retired PC can give out blessings. Each cycle, a PC can ask your retired PC to give them any one blessing from the list in the crypt institution. The blessing doesn’t count against the number of blessings a PC can have and is bestowed as if it were bestowed by a 5th-level crypt.

- **Shopaholic:** Your retired PC lowers the amount of money the PCs need to spend to increase the village’s Prosperity each cycle by 1,000 gc.

- **Work With My Hands:** Your retired PC goes to work at an artisan institution in the village. This institution completes custom crafting projects 3 days quicker. It still takes a minimum of 1 day to craft an item.
