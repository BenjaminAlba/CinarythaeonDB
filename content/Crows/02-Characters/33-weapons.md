# Weapons

> Source: Crows — Character Booklet, Playtest 3, PDF page(s) 40–45.

A weapon’s equipment card shows how much damage it deals on a tier 2 or 3 result with an attack. It doesn’t show the tier 1 result, which is always a miss.

The Weapons table shows the price of each weapon, the number of slots it takes up, its type, its range, and its qualities. Weapons can be purchased at the blacksmith institution. Blacksmithing is the expertise used to make weapons.

### Weapon Types

A weapon’s type determines the expertise that can be applied to attacks made with it. A weapon can a be bashing, bow, chopping, slashing, stabbing, or unarmed weapon.

### Ammunition

Bow weapons use ammunition. Arrows for longbows and shortbows. Bolts for crossbows. To attack with a bow weapon, you must have its ammunition in one of your belt slots.

### Weapon Qualities

A weapon can have any of the following qualities:

- **Brutal:** When you get a crit on an attack with this weapon, you deal twice as much damage with the attack as your normally would.

- **Cumbersome:** The weapon only occupies 1 slot on your backpack or belt but requires 2 hand slots to wield.

- **Disengage:** When you take the Shift maneuver while wielding this weapon, you can move 1 additional square as part of the maneuver. If you wield two weapons with this quality, the benefits stack.

- **Dismember:** When you score a crit on an attack made with this weapon, it removes one of the target’s limbs. Roll on the Dismember table to determine the limb removed.

- **Heavy:** Upgrading this weapon’s materials costs more and has greater benefits than other weapons. See the Heavy Weapons Upgraded table for more information.

- **Light:** When you hit with a melee attack while wielding two light weapons, you deal additional damage equal to the unused weapon’s tier 2 result (without the inclusion of your Agility or

Strength as part of the damage). While a hand slot is empty, it counts as a light weapon that can be used to make unarmed strikes.

- **Parry X:** While you wield this weapon, you can use it to absorb damage as if it were a shield. The weapon has AD equivalent to X. When the weapon’s AD is 0, it takes a -1 damage penalty. You can repair the weapon as if it were a shield using the Repair Armor rest activity.

- **Pummeling:** When you get a tier 3 result with an attack made with this weapon against a creature who is your size or smaller, you can push them 1 square. When you get a crit on an attack with this weapon, you can knock a target of your size or smaller prone.

- **Reload:** You must use a maneuver to load 1 piece of ammunition into this weapon before it can be used to attack. After being used to attack, it must be loaded again before it can be used to make another attack.

**Dismember**

| d6 | Limb and Effect* |
| --- | --- |

If the creature has no arms remaining, they can’t make any attacks or complete tasks that require arms. |
| 3-4 | Leg: The attacker chooses which leg is removed. The creature’s speed is reduced proportionally. If the creature has no legs remaining, they can’t stand and their speed is 0. |

*Some creatures have only legs. Others have limbs that serve as both arms and legs. When you remove a limb from such a creature, the Ref determines which effects on the table are appropriate, combining or adjusting as they see fit. If a creature doesn’t have discernible anatomy, you instead deal twice as much damage against them as you normally would with a crit rather than dismembering them.

### Crafting Standard Weapons

All standard weapons and 20 standard arrows or bolts on the weapons table have a crafting complexity of 1 and a crafting goal of 5. Blacksmithing is the expertise required to make all weapons and ammunition. Bow weapons require 1 hickory log as a crafting material. All other weapons and 20 arrows or bolts requires 1 iron bar.

### Upgraded Weapons

Ammunition and weapons that aren’t bows or unarmed can have their damage increased by using better metal to create them and whether or not they are heavy weapons. Bows can have their range increased by using better wood in their construction. The weapons upgraded tables show each upgrade’s bonus and price (which is added to the item’s base cost to get the total), and the complexity, materials, and crafting goal required to make weapons and ammunition from these materials. Blacksmithing is the expertise used to make upgraded weapons.

### Weapon Enchantments

Weapon enchantments can be added to existing weapons. The Weapon Enchantments table shows the complexity, materials, and crafting goal required to enchant a weapon. Enchanting is the expertise required to add enchantments to weapons.

The table also shows the price for adding an enchantment to a weapon. If you buy a weapon with an enchantment, add its price to the weapon’s price.

A weapon can have multiple enchantments. The total of all the enchantments’ complexities can’t exceed 4.

Weapon enchantments can be purchased at the enchanter institution.

- **Absorbing:** When you get a crit on an attack with this weapon against a creature, you can choose one attack from the creature’s stat block and make that attack using the creature’s stats this turn. When you get a doom on an attack made with this weapon, you can’t make attacks with it until the end of your next combat turn.

- **Dancing:** As a maneuver while wielding this weapon, you command it to float in the air and follow and fight for you, freeing it from your hand slot(s) though you are still considered wielding it, until the end of the DT. A creature who scores a crit against you while the weapon is floating can grab it and wield it (no action required), causing the weapon to stop floating. If the weapon is ever more than 1 square from you, it stops floating. The weapon can’t be made to float again until you finish a rest. You can only have one dancing weapon active at a time.

- **Defending:** The weapon gains the Parry 5 quality or if it already has the quality, it increases by 5. When the weapon’s AD is 0, it takes a -2 damage penalty.

- **Exploding:** When you kill a creature with this weapon, they explode. Each creature within 1 square of them takes 1d6 damage. If the creature already explodes on death, any damage they deal is increased by 1d6. When you get a doom on an attack with this weapon, a mundane item you carry (chosen by the Ref) is destroyed.

- **Flaming:** As a maneuver while wielding this weapon, you can command it to ignite in a magical fire that gives the weapon a +2 damage bonus and causes it to shed light 5/5. You can use another maneuver to extinguish the flames. When you get a doom on an attack with this weapon, you take 1d10 damage as the weapon suddenly bursts with flame and then its fire goes out and you can’t ignite it again until the end of the DT.

- **Frosty:** When you get a tier 3 result on an attack with this weapon against a creature, the creature’s speed is reduced by 1. This effect can’t reduce a creature’s speed below 2. When you get a doom result on an attack with this weapon, your speed is reduced to 2 (if it isn’t already lower) until the end of the DT.

- **Gashing:** When you get a tier 3 result on an attack with this weapon against a creature, the creature gains a bleeding gash. The gash deals 1d6 P damage to a creature at the start of their combat turns. If target recieves magic healing or the gash’s damage result is 6, the gash stops dealing damage to the target. When get a doom on an attack with the weapon, you get a gash.

- **Hewing:** This enchantment can only be added to non-bow weapons. As an action, you can use this weapon to make a hole in a wall, floor, or ceiling that has a circumference of 1d10 feet and a depth of 1d10 feet. This effect has 1 UD rolled on use and recharges on rest. When the UD are 0, you are vulnerable.

- **Hungry:** When you kill a creature with this weapon, you gain 3 AD. AD gained this way disappears at the end of the DT. If you wield the weapon in a combat encounter and the encounter ends without you having killed anything with it, you take 1d6 P damage.

- **Impact:** When you get a tier 3 result on an attack with this weapon and the target isn’t force moved, you can push the target 1 square. If the target is already force moved by the attack, you can force move them 1 additional square. If you get a doom on an attack with this weapon, the Ref slides you 1d10 squares.

- **Infinity:** This enchantment can only be added to bow weapons. If you don’t load ammunition into this weapon, it generates its own phantom ammunition that can be used to make attacks. The ammunition has a -1 damage penalty.

- **Lightning:** When you hit a target with an attack with this weapon, you can choose a second target within 3 squares of the first. That creature also takes the attack’s damage. This effect has 1 UD rolled on use and recharges on rest. When the UD are 0, you are weakened.

- **Poisoning:** As a maneuver, you can command this weapon to drip poison, an effect that has 1 UD to be rolled on use and which recharges on rest. If the next attack you make with your weapon is a hit, the target is weakened. If you miss with your next attack, you are vulnerable.

- **Raging:** When you kill another creature with this weapon, you can use a reaction to enter a rage that lasts until the end of the DT. While in the rage, you gain a +2 bonus to damage on attacks with this weapon and a +1 bonus to speed while you wield this weapon. When the rage ends, you are weakened.

- **Returning:** This enchantment can only be added to non-bow weapons. The weapon’s ranged attack range increases by 5 and after you use it to make a ranged attack, the weapon return to your hand slot(s). If you get a doom on a ranged attack made with the weapon, it deals its tier 3 damage to you when it returns.

- **Slaying:** You choose a specific creature type when you place this enchantment on a weapon.

The weapon gains a +1 bonus on attacks against creatures of that type and a -1 penalty on attacks against all other creatures. You can only place one slaying enchantment on a weapon.

- **Sworn Foe:** As a maneuver while wielding this weapon, you can choose a creature within 10 squares of you. You deal an additional 2 damage on attacks with this weapon against that creature. If that creature counters you, you take 2 additional damage. If you choose a new creature, the effects of this weapon no longer apply to the old creature.

- **Teleporting:** When you deal damage to another creature with this weapon, it gains 1d6 charges. As a maneuver, you can expend all charges from it and teleport up to a number of squares equal to the charges spent. If the charges reach 12 or higher, they are all expended and you take P damage equal to the charges spent. All charges in the armor disappear at the end of a DT.

- **Vicious:** When you get a crit on an attack with this weapon, you deal an additional 1d10 damage. When you get a doom on an attack with this weapon, you take 1d10 P damage.

- **Weakening:** When you hit a creature with a tier 3 attack made with this weapon and do so again before the end of your next combat turn, the creature is weakened. When you get a doom on an attack with this weapon, you are weakened.
