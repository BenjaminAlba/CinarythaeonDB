# Atraphothoromancia

## Summary

In combat, if you have this resonance, you can empower an Atraphothoromancia spell that directly targets a creature: spend one of your Hit Point Dice and roll a d8, d10, or d12. One target takes extra damage equal to the roll plus your spellcasting modifier, even if the spell misses or the target saves. Use the spell's damage type, or Force if it deals no damage. Afterward, you lose Hit Points equal to the roll.

## Resonance: Bloodletting

*Blood, power, and sacrifice.*

Atraphothoromancia draws power from the caster's willingness to wound and exhaust their own body. Each sacrifice strengthens the spell at the cost of the vitality needed to endure what follows.

While you have resonance with Atraphothoromancia, you gain the following benefit.

***Blood Sacrifice.*** During combat, when you cast an Atraphothoromancia spell that directly targets one or more creatures, you can expend one of your unspent Hit Point Dice without rolling it. Then choose and roll a d8, d10, or d12; this is your sacrifice die. The expended Hit Point Die restores no Hit Points. Pay the spell's normal costs and obey its casting limits.

A spell that targets only an area, point, or object doesn't qualify, even if creatures are in the area.

***Bloodletting Damage.*** After the spell's initial effects resolve, one directly targeted creature of your choice takes extra damage equal to the sacrifice die's result plus your spellcasting ability modifier for the spell (minimum 1). Apply it once per casting, even if the spell has multiple targets or attacks. It applies even if the spell deals no damage, misses, or the target succeeds on its saving throw.

The damage has a type dealt by the spell, chosen by you if it has several types; if the spell deals no damage, use Force. Resolve the spell's normal damage first. Apply Resistance, Immunity, and Vulnerability to the extra damage normally; the spell's successful save doesn't reduce it.

If the spell's initial casting also damaged that creature, the extra damage is part of its last damage event from that casting. Otherwise, it is a separate damage event.

***Price of Blood.*** After the spell and extra damage resolve, you lose Hit Points equal to the sacrifice die's result. This loss isn't damage: Temporary Hit Points don't absorb it, and it doesn't require a Concentration saving throw. It can reduce you to 0 Hit Points; if you become Incapacitated, you lose Concentration as normal.
