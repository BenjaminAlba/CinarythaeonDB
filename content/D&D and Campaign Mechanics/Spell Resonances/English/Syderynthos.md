# Syderynthos

## Summary

In combat, if you have this resonance, once per turn after casting a Syderynthos spell, you can move yourself or a creature you can see within 30 feet up to 5 feet horizontally. It must be Large or smaller, and an ally must be willing. If an enemy moved this way leaves an ally's reach, one such ally can use a Reaction to make an Opportunity Attack against it.

## Resonance: Aftershock

*Transmutation, combustion, and unstable matter.*

Syderynthos forces matter into sudden change, releasing unstable energy that displaces creatures and creates fleeting openings for allies.

While you have resonance with Syderynthos, you gain the following benefit.

***Aftershock.*** Once per turn during combat, immediately after a Syderynthos spell you cast resolves its initial effects, choose yourself or one creature you can see within 30 feet. It must be Large or smaller, and an ally must be willing to be moved. Move it up to 5 feet horizontally into an unoccupied space it can occupy. This is forced movement.

If an enemy moved by Aftershock leaves the reach of one or more of your allies who can see it, choose one of those allies. Despite the forced movement, the enemy provokes an Opportunity Attack from that ally immediately before leaving its reach. The ally can use its Reaction as normal. Only one ally can be chosen per use; features responding to a provoked Opportunity Attack, such as War Caster, work normally.

Only Aftershock's movement gains this exception; the spell's own movement follows its normal rules. Aftershock needs no additional action, Reaction, or save from you. It can activate on another creature's turn, including from a Reaction spell; the limit applies separately to each turn.

The spell needn't deal damage, require a save, or target the chosen creature. The benefit works even if the spell misses or a target succeeds on its save. Later spell effects don't activate it again.
