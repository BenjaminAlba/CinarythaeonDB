# Nihilokathra

## Summary

In combat, if you have this resonance, once per turn a Nihilokathra spell you cast can give 1 Corruption stack to a creature after damaging it. When a corrupted creature takes damage, its existing stacks add 1 Force damage each, ignoring Resistance and Vulnerability. At the end of each of its turns, it saves to remove all stacks, gaining +2 to later recovery saves each time it fails.

## Resonance: Corruption

*Hatred, corruption, and the dissolution of order.*

Nihilokathra arises from hatred, wrath, and the conscious desire to inflict harm. Its magic erodes the integrity of its victims, allowing each successive wound to deepen their ruin.

While you have resonance with Nihilokathra, you gain the following benefit.

***Inflict Corruption.*** Once per turn during combat, when a Nihilokathra spell you cast deals damage to one or more creatures, you can give one of them 1 Corruption stack. Apply the stack after the damage is resolved; a creature that takes no damage can't gain a stack from that event. Each character has their own once-per-turn limit, including on other creatures' turns.

***Corrupted Wounds.*** Whenever a creature with Corruption takes damage, that damage event deals extra Force damage equal to its current stacks. This extra damage ignores Resistance and Vulnerability; Immunity to Force applies normally. It is part of the triggering event, can't trigger itself or apply a stack, and doesn't consume stacks. Damage that would otherwise be 0 doesn't trigger it.

Each separate hit or later occurrence of damage can trigger this benefit. Multiple damage types from one hit count as one event, as does damage dealt simultaneously by a single effect. Existing stacks increase the damage that applies a new stack; the new stack doesn't increase that damage.

***Ending Corruption.*** At the end of each of its turns, a corrupted creature makes a Constitution saving throw, even if it gained its first stack that turn. The DC is the spell save DC of the creature that applied the first stack of its current Corruption; it remains fixed until that Corruption ends.

On a success, all the creature's stacks end. On a failure, it gains a cumulative +2 bonus to later saves to end that Corruption. Stacks from different characters share one uncapped total. Adding stacks changes neither the DC nor the recovery bonus and doesn't delay the save.

Corruption and its recovery bonus end when combat ends. If Corruption is applied again after ending, use the new first applier's DC and start without a recovery bonus.
