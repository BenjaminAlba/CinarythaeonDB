# Oboromancia

## Summary

In combat, if you have this resonance, once per turn after casting an Oboromancia spell, you can choose: create a barrier beside yourself or a visible ally within 30 feet that grants Half Cover from the other side until it protects against an attack or Dexterity save, or until the start of your next turn; or, if you spent a spell slot, use one nonmagical object with the Utilize action without spending an action.

## Resonance: Mutable Matter

*Transmutation, alchemy, and the alteration of matter.*

Oboromancia arises from curiosity about the composition of the Æther and the material world. Its practitioners reshape matter in fleeting moments, turning their surroundings and equipment to their advantage.

While you have resonance with Oboromancia, you gain the following benefit.

***Mutable Matter.*** Once per turn during combat, immediately after an Oboromancia spell you cast resolves its initial effects, choose one effect below.

***Quick Handling.*** If you expended a spell slot to cast the spell, immediately take the Utilize action to use one nonmagical object without an action, Bonus Action, or additional Reaction. Meet the object's usual requirements for reach, hands, proficiency, and checks, and expend its uses or other required resources normally. Pact Magic slots qualify.

Only object uses that require the Utilize action qualify; this doesn't grant the Attack or Magic action or an item use that requires a Bonus Action. A spell cast without a slot can't use this option.

***Reshaped Cover.*** Choose yourself or one ally you can see within 30 feet. A 5-foot-wide barrier appears beside that creature, granting it Half Cover while it remains within 5 feet and an attack or other effect comes from the opposite side. The barrier protects only that creature, doesn't block movement or line of sight, and can't grant more than Half Cover.

If an attack roll is made against the creature, or it makes a Dexterity saving throw, while it is within 5 feet and the barrier is between it and the source, the barrier applies to that roll and disappears after it resolves, whatever the result. Other saves and effects from the same side don't end it. It also disappears at the start of your next turn or when combat ends, whichever comes first.

The options share one use per turn, including other creatures' turns and spells cast as a Reaction. The spell needn't deal damage, require a save, or target the protected creature; the benefit works even if the spell misses or a target succeeds on its save. Later spell effects don't activate it again.
