# Mnelyxphora

## Summary

In combat, if you have this resonance, once per turn after casting a Mnelyxphora spell, you can choose yourself or a visible ally within 30 feet. Before your next turn, when an attack hits it or a spell affects it and damage dice are involved, subtract the highest die result from its damage and deal that full result as Force damage to a visible enemy within 30 feet—even if the chosen creature takes no damage.

## Resonance: Stolen Wound

*Memory, transgression, and stolen consequences.*

Mnelyxphora reaches into the traces that events leave behind, making a wound belong to a creature other than the one it struck.

While you have resonance with Mnelyxphora, you gain the following benefit.

***Stolen Wound.*** Once per turn during combat, immediately after a Mnelyxphora spell you cast resolves its initial effects, choose yourself or one ally you can see within 30 feet. Before the start of your next turn, the next time an attack hits that creature or it is subjected to a spell's damaging effect, if damage dice are part of that attack or spell effect, this benefit triggers. A spell attack must hit.

When it triggers, choose one enemy you can see within 30 feet. Find the highest number rolled on any single damage die for that attack or spell effect. After the chosen creature's save and defenses, reduce its damage by that number (minimum 0), before subtracting it from Temporary Hit Points or Hit Points. The enemy takes Force damage equal to the full die result, even if the reduction was smaller or 0. The enemy's Force defenses apply normally; other effects of the triggering attack or spell are unchanged.

The benefit triggers only if you can see an enemy within 30 feet. Otherwise it waits until it triggers or the start of your next turn. It ends when it triggers, at the start of your next turn, or when combat ends.

***Damage Rolls.*** A successful save, Immunity, or another effect reducing the chosen creature's damage to 0 doesn't prevent this benefit. If its damage dice aren't normally rolled, roll them to find the highest result—for example, when a save prevents all damage or the DM uses a monster's listed average.

A missed attack, fixed damage without dice, or damage from a source that is neither an attack nor a spell doesn't trigger it. For a shared roll, reduce only the chosen creature's damage. The enemy's Force damage is a separate event and can't trigger Stolen Wound.

This benefit requires no additional action or Reaction and can activate on another creature's turn, including from a Reaction spell. The Mnelyxphora spell needn't deal damage, require a save, or target the chosen creature; its later effects don't activate this benefit again.

***Example.*** If the highest die is 6 and the ally would take 3 damage after defenses, the ally takes 0 and the enemy takes 6 Force damage, subject to its defenses.
