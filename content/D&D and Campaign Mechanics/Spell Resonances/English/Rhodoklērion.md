# Rhodoklērion

## Summary

In combat, if you have this resonance, once per turn when you cast a Rhodoklērion spell, you can choose one visible enemy within 30 feet or directly targeted by the spell. After the spell resolves, either prevent it from making Opportunity Attacks until the start of your next turn, or grant Advantage to the next attack roll made against it by an ally other than you before then.

## Resonance: Misdirection

*Perception, beauty, and emotional manipulation.*

Rhodoklērion bends attention through beauty, deception, and emotion. Its practitioners, the magicians of roses, use fleeting illusions and altered perceptions to create openings for themselves and their allies.

While you have resonance with Rhodoklērion, you gain the following benefit.

***Misdirection.*** Once per turn during combat, when you cast a Rhodoklērion spell, choose one hostile creature you can see that is within 30 feet of you or directly targeted by the spell, regardless of distance. Choose one effect below. It begins after the spell's initial effects resolve and requires no additional action or saving throw.

The spell needn't deal damage or require a save; the benefit works even if the spell misses or a target succeeds on its save. Only one creature gains the effect, and it ends when combat ends.

- ***Distracting Display.*** The creature can't make Opportunity Attacks until the start of your next turn. It can take other Reactions.
- ***False Opening.*** Before the start of your next turn, the next attack roll against the creature by an ally other than you has Advantage. The effect ends after that roll, hit or miss, or at the start of your next turn.
