# Viridexacion

## Summary

In combat, if you have this resonance, once per turn when you cast a Viridexacion spell, choose yourself or a visible ally within 30 feet. The willing creature can spend one of its Hit Point Dice; you can spend one of an Unconscious ally's dice on its behalf. Roll it and add its Constitution modifier to restore Hit Points or grant Temporary Hit Points until combat ends.

## Resonance: Shared Vitality

*Nature, vitality, and balance.*

Viridexacion arises from a connection to the material and spiritual worlds. Its magic calls upon a creature's own reserves of vitality to mend wounds and preserve life.

While you have resonance with Viridexacion, you gain the following benefit.

***Shared Vitality.*** Once per turn during combat, when you cast a Viridexacion spell, choose yourself or one willing or Unconscious ally you can see within 30 feet. After the spell's initial effects resolve, that creature can expend one of its unspent Hit Point Dice. You can expend an Unconscious ally's die on its behalf.

Roll the expended die and add the chosen creature's Constitution modifier (minimum total of 1). Choose one effect:

- ***Renewal.*** The creature regains Hit Points equal to the total.
- ***Shelter.*** The creature gains Temporary Hit Points equal to the total. They last until depleted or combat ends, whichever comes first.

This benefit uses no additional action or Reaction and expends only the chosen creature's die. One creature can expend one die per casting. The spell needn't heal or target that creature; later effects of the spell don't activate this benefit again.

Renewal can restore Hit Points to an Unconscious creature at 0 Hit Points, ending that condition as normal. Shelter doesn't restore consciousness.
