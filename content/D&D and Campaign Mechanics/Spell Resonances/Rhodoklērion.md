# Rhodoklērion
*Perception, beauty, and emotional manipulation.*

Rhodoklērion bends attention through beauty, deception, and emotion. Its practitioners, the magicians of roses, use fleeting illusions and altered perceptions to create openings for themselves and their allies.

## Resonance: Misdirection

While you have resonance with Rhodoklērion, you gain the following benefit.

***Misdirection.*** Once per turn during combat, when you cast a Rhodoklērion spell, you can choose one hostile creature you can see. The creature must be within 30 feet of you or directly targeted by the spell, even if it is more than 30 feet away. Choose one of the following effects for that creature. The effect begins after the spell's initial effects resolve and requires no additional action or saving throw.

The spell needn't deal damage or require a saving throw. You can use this benefit even if the spell misses or a target succeeds on a saving throw against it. Only one creature gains the chosen effect, even if the spell targets or affects more than one creature.

***Distracting Display.*** Until the start of your next turn, the chosen creature can't make Opportunity Attacks. It can still take other Reactions.

***False Opening.*** The next attack roll made against the chosen creature by one of your allies other than yourself has Advantage, provided the roll is made before the start of your next turn. The effect ends after that roll, whether the attack hits or misses, or at the start of your next turn, whichever comes first.

## Combat Only

All resonance effects function only during combat. You can't use Misdirection outside combat, and its effects end when combat ends.
