# Oboromancia
*Transmutation, alchemy, and the alteration of matter.*

Oboromancia arises from curiosity about the composition of the Æther and the material world. Its practitioners reshape matter in fleeting moments, turning their surroundings and equipment to their advantage.

## Resonance: Mutable Matter

While you have resonance with Oboromancia, you gain the following benefit.

***Mutable Matter.*** Once per turn during combat, immediately after the initial effects of an Oboromancia spell you cast resolve, you can choose one of the following effects.

***Quick Handling.*** If you expended a spell slot to cast the spell, you can immediately take the Utilize action to use one nonmagical object without expending an action, a Bonus Action, or an additional Reaction. You must meet the object's normal requirements, including any requirements for reach, hands, proficiency, or ability checks. Expend any uses or other resources the object requires as normal.

Only a use that normally requires the Utilize action qualifies. This benefit doesn't allow you to take the Attack or Magic action or use an item that normally requires a Bonus Action. A Pact Magic spell slot qualifies for this benefit; casting a spell without expending a spell slot doesn't.

***Reshaped Cover.*** Choose yourself or one ally you can see within 30 feet of you. A 5-foot-wide barrier of reshaped material appears beside the chosen creature. While the creature is within 5 feet of the barrier, it has Half Cover against attacks and other effects that originate on the opposite side of the barrier.

The barrier grants cover only to the chosen creature. It doesn't block movement or line of sight, provide cover to other creatures, or grant more than Half Cover.

If an attack roll is made against the chosen creature, or the creature makes a Dexterity saving throw, and the barrier is between the creature and the origin of the attack or effect while the creature is within 5 feet of the barrier, the barrier disappears immediately after the roll is resolved. The cover applies to that attack or saving throw before the barrier disappears, whether the attack hits or misses or the saving throw succeeds or fails.

The barrier also disappears at the start of your next turn or when combat ends, whichever comes first. A saving throw other than a Dexterity saving throw, or an attack or effect originating on the same side of the barrier as the creature, doesn't cause the barrier to disappear.

***Using the Benefit.*** The once-per-turn limit applies to both effects together. You can use this benefit on another creature's turn, including when you cast a spell as a Reaction.

The spell needn't deal damage, require a saving throw, or target the creature you choose for Reshaped Cover. You can use this benefit even if the spell misses or a creature succeeds on a saving throw against it. The spell's later effects don't activate this benefit again.

## Combat Only

All resonance effects function only during combat. You can't use Mutable Matter outside combat, and a barrier created by Reshaped Cover disappears when combat ends.
