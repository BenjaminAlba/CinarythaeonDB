# Nihilokathra
*Hatred, corruption, and the dissolution of order.*

Nihilokathra arises from hatred, wrath, and the conscious desire to inflict harm. Its magic erodes the integrity of its victims, allowing each successive wound to deepen their ruin.

## Resonance: Corruption

While you have resonance with Nihilokathra, you gain the following benefit.

***Inflict Corruption.*** Once per turn, when a Nihilokathra spell you cast deals damage to one or more creatures, you can give one of those creatures 1 stack of Corruption. Apply the stack after resolving the triggering damage. A creature that takes no damage from the spell cannot receive a stack from that damage event.

This limit applies separately to each character inflicting Corruption. You can apply a stack on another creature's turn if your spell deals damage on that turn and you haven't used this benefit during that turn.

***Accumulating Corruption.*** A creature's Corruption stacks have no maximum. Stacks applied by different characters contribute to the same total. Adding stacks does not reset the creature's recovery bonus or change its recovery save DC, as described below.

***Corrupted Wounds.*** Whenever a creature with Corruption takes damage, that damage event deals additional Force damage equal to its current number of Corruption stacks. Resistance and Vulnerability do not modify this additional damage.

The additional damage is part of the triggering damage event, not a separate event. It cannot trigger itself or apply Corruption. It does not consume stacks. A damage event that would otherwise deal no damage does not trigger this benefit.

Each separate hit or subsequent occurrence of damage can trigger this benefit. Multiple damage types dealt by the same hit count as one event. Damage delivered simultaneously by a single effect also counts as one event. A newly applied stack does not increase the damage that applied it, but existing stacks can increase that damage normally.

***Resist Corruption.*** At the end of each of its turns, a creature with Corruption makes a Constitution saving throw. The DC equals the spell save DC of the creature that applied the first stack of its current Corruption. This DC remains fixed until that Corruption ends.

On a successful save, the creature loses all its Corruption stacks. On a failed save, it gains a cumulative +2 bonus to subsequent saving throws made to end that Corruption. This bonus applies only to these recovery saves.

The creature makes this save even if it first received Corruption during the current turn. Additional stacks do not delay the save. When its Corruption ends, its accumulated recovery bonus ends as well. If it later receives Corruption again, determine a new DC from the spell applying the first new stack, with no accumulated recovery bonus.

## Combat Only

All resonance effects function only during combat. You cannot apply Corruption outside combat, and all Corruption stacks and associated recovery bonuses end when combat ends.
