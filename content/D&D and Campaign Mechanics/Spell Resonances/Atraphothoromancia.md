# Atraphothoromancia
*Blood, power, and sacrifice.*

Atraphothoromancia draws power from the caster's willingness to wound and exhaust their own body. Each sacrifice strengthens the spell at the cost of the vitality needed to endure what follows.

## Resonance: Bloodletting

While you have resonance with Atraphothoromancia, you gain the following benefit.

***Blood Sacrifice.*** During combat, when you cast an Atraphothoromancia spell that directly targets one or more creatures, you can expend one of your unspent Hit Point Dice to empower the spell. Choose and roll a d8, d10, or d12. This die is your *sacrifice die* for that casting.

The Hit Point Die you expend is not rolled and restores no Hit Points. You must also expend any spell slot or other resource required to cast the spell, and the normal casting limits apply.

***Bloodletting Damage.*** After the spell's initial effects resolve, choose one creature directly targeted by the spell. It takes additional damage equal to the sacrifice die's result plus your spellcasting ability modifier for the spell, with a minimum of 1 damage.

This damage applies once per casting, to only one creature, even if the spell targets multiple creatures or makes multiple attacks. It applies even if the spell normally deals no damage, its attack misses, or the target succeeds on its saving throw.

You can use Blood Sacrifice only if the spell directly targets a creature. A spell that targets only an area, point in space, or object doesn't qualify, even if creatures are within that area.

***Damage Type and Timing.*** If the spell deals damage, the additional damage has one damage type the spell deals, chosen by you if it deals more than one type. If the spell deals no damage, the additional damage is Force.

Calculate the spell's normal damage before applying Bloodletting damage. The target's Resistance, Immunity, and Vulnerability apply normally to the Bloodletting damage itself. A successful saving throw against the spell doesn't reduce the Bloodletting damage.

If the chosen creature also takes damage from the spell's initial casting, treat the Bloodletting damage as part of the last damage event from that casting against the creature. If the creature otherwise takes no damage from the casting, the Bloodletting damage is one damage event of its own.

***Price of Blood.*** After the spell and its Bloodletting damage resolve, you lose Hit Points equal to the sacrifice die's result. This is Hit Point loss, not damage: Temporary Hit Points can't absorb it, and it doesn't itself require a Concentration saving throw.

This loss can reduce you to 0 Hit Points. If it does, the spell's initial effects and Bloodletting damage have already resolved. If you become Incapacitated, you lose Concentration as normal.

## Combat Only

All resonance effects function only during combat. You cannot use Blood Sacrifice outside combat.
