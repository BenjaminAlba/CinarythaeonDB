# Blood Hunter
Blood hunters are clever warriors driven by an unending determination to destroy evils old and new. Armed with rites of secretive [[Atraphothoromancia|Atraphothoromancy]] and a willingness to sacrifice their own vitality for their cause, they protect the realms from the shadows —even as they remain ever vigilant against being drawn to the darkness that consumes the evils they hunt.

Through careful study and practice, blood hunters hone the rites of [[Atraphothoromancia|Atraphothoromancy]] into unique combat techniques, forfeiting a portion of their own health to call blood curses down upon their enemies or summon the elements to aid their strikes. Willing to suffer whatever it takes to achieve victory, these adept warriors have forged themselves into a potent force dedicated to protecting the innocent.

## Core Blood Hunter Traits

Strenght / Dexterity and Wisdom / Intelligence

1d10 per blood hunter level

## Class Features

### Hemocraft (Level 1)

You have survived the Hunter’s Bane —a dangerous, long-guarded ritual that alters your life’s blood, forever binding you to the darkness and honing your senses against it. You have advantage on Wisdom ([[Abilities#Survival|Survival]]) checks to track fey, fiends, or undead, as well as on Intelligence checks to recall information about such creatures.

The Hunter’s Bane also empowers your body to control and shape [[Atraphothoromancia|Atraphothoromancy]] magic, using your own blood and life essence to fuel your abilities. Some of your features require your target to make a saving throw to resist the feature’s effects. The saving throw DC ([[Blood Hunter#Hemocraft save DC|Hemocraft save]]) is calculated as follows:

***Hemocraft save DC***
8 + your proficiency bonus + your [[Blood Hunter#Hemocraft modifier|Hemocraft modifier]].

***Hemocraft modifier***
Your choice between your Intelligence or Wisdom modifiers.

***Blood Curses.*** You learn 2 blood curses from the Blood Curse Options list. You learn an additional blood curse at Blood Hunter level 6, 10, 14 and 18. Each time you learn a new blood curse, you can also choose one of the blood curses you know and replace it with another blood curse. Your subclass also grants you a special blood curse at a later level.

***Number of Uses.*** You can use this feature to invoke your blood curses twice, choosing which effect to use from those you know. You regain all expended uses when you finish a Short or Long Rest. You gain an additional use when you reach Blood Hunter level 6 (3 uses), 13 (4 uses), and 17 (5 uses).

***Amplify.*** Blood curses can consume life essence to produce stronger effects. When you use your Hemocraft to invoke a blood curse, you can amplify it and lose a number of hit points equal to one roll of your hemocraft die, and apply the amplify effect onto the target of your blood curse. The first time you amplify a blood curse after finishing a Short or Long Rest, you gain the amount of hit points lost as temporary hit points.

### Weapon Mastery (Level 1)

Your training with weapons allows you to use the mastery properties of two kinds of weapons of your choice with which you have proficiency, such as Shortswords and Light Crossbows.

Whenever you finish a Long Rest, you can change the kinds of weapons you chose. For example, you could switch to using the mastery properties of Whips and Handaxes.

### Blood Magic (Level 1)

The rites of the Hunter's Bane teach you to channel magic through your blood and life essence. You gain the following benefits.

**Blood Hunter Cantrips.** You know two cantrips of your choice from the Blood Hunter spell list. [[Blood Bolt]] and [[Vengeful Blade]] are recommended. These cantrips are in addition to those you gain from other spellcasting features. Whenever you finish a Long Rest, you can replace one cantrip learned from this feature with another Blood Hunter cantrip of your choice.

**Blood Hunter Spells.** You always have the spells on the Blood Hunter spell list prepared. You have a spell from this feature prepared only if a Spellcasting or Pact Magic feature grants you a spell slot of that spell's level or higher.

These spells are Blood Hunter spells for you, and they don't count against the number of spells you can prepare with another feature. You can cast them using any spell slots you have of the required level or higher.

**Spellcasting Ability.** Your Hemocraft modifier is your spellcasting ability for the Blood Hunter spells you gain from this feature and Sanguine Arcanum.

**Spellcasting Focus.** You can use an Arcane Focus, Alchemist's Supplies, or a weapon for which you have an active Crimson Rite as a Spellcasting Focus.

### Fighting Style (Level 2)

You gain a Fighting Style feat of your choice. Instead of choosing one of those feats, you can choose the option below.

***Mutagenic Warrior.*** You learn two mutagen formulae from the Order of the Mutant subclass. You can concoct a single mutagen from the formulae you know with your Alchemist's Supplies when you finish a Long Rest, which become inert when you finish a Long Rest. You can consume the mutagen as a Bonus Action, which lasts until you finish a Short or Long Rest.

You gain this feature again at Blood Hunter level 6.

### Crimson Rite (Level 2)

You learn to invoke a rite of [[Atraphothoromancia|Atraphothoromancy]] that infuses your weapon strikes with elemental energy. Choose two Crimson Rites from the list below. You choose another crimson rite at Blood Hunter level 7 and 14. Whenever you gain a Crimson Rite, you can replace one of your Crimson Rite options with another.

As a bonus action, you can activate a crimson rite you know onto any number of weapons you are holding. When you do so, you lose hit points equal to one roll of your hemocraft die.

While the rite is activated and you wield the weapon, the weapon's attacks deal extra damage equal to your hemocraft die. The damage type is determined by the rite you choose to activate. Each weapon can only have one rite active, and the rite lasts until the end of a Short or Long Rest, if you choose to activate a different rite on a weapon with the rite active, or if you dismiss the rite. 

**Rite of the Flame.** The extra damage dealt by your rite is fire damage.
**Rite of the Frozen.** The extra damage dealt by your rite is cold damage.
**Rite of the Storm.** The extra damage dealt by your rite is lightning damage.
**Rite of the Dead.** The extra damage dealt by your rite is necrotic damage.
**Rite of the Oracle.** The extra damage dealt by your rite is psychic damage.
**Rite of the Roar.** The extra damage dealt by your rite is thunder damage.

### Blood Hunter Subclass (Level 3)

You gain a Blood Hunter subclass of your choice. A subclass is a specialization that grants you features at certain Blood Hunter levels. For the rest of your career, you gain each of your subclass's features that are of your Blood Hunter level or lower.

### Ability Score Improvement (Level 4)

You gain the [[Feats#Ability Score Improvement|Ability Score Improvement]] feat or another [[Feats|feat]] of your choice for which you qualify. You gain this feature again at Blood Hunter levels 8, 12, and 16.

### Extra Attack (Level 5)

You can attack twice instead of once whenever you take the Attack action on your turn.

### Brand of Castigation (Level 6)

When you damage a creature with a weapon for which you have an active crimson rite, you can channel [[Atraphothoromancia]] magic to sear an arcane brand into that creature (no action required). You always know the direction to the branded creature as long as it’s on the same plane as you. Further, each time the branded creature deals damage to you or a creature you can see within 10 feet of you, the branded creature takes psychic damage equal to your Hemocraft Modifier

Your brand lasts until you dismiss it or until you use this feature to apply a brand to another creature. Your brand can be dispelled with [[Dispel Magic]], and is treated as a spell with a level equal to half your blood hunter level (maximum 9th level).

When the branded creature dies while on the same plane as you, you can benefit from one of the following effects of your choice:

***Breathe the Soul.*** You regain one expended use of your Hemocraft. 

***Consume the Flesh.*** You regain hit points equal to two rolls of your Hemocraft die plus your Hemocraft modifier.

***Drink the Blood.*** You end any one condition or magical effect that is currently affecting you.

You can use this feature twice. You regain one of its expended uses when you finish a Short Rest, and you
regain all expended uses when you finish a Long Rest.

### Grim Psychometry (Level 9)

You gain a supernatural talent for discerning the secrets surrounding mysterious relics or places touched by evil. Whenever you make a [[Abilities#History|history]] check to recall information about the sinister or tragic history of an object you are touching or your current location, you have advantage on the check. At the DM’s discretion, a suitably high roll might cause your character to experience brief visions of the past connected to the object or location.

Aditionally, you have the [[Identify]] spell prepared. You can cast this spell as a Bonus Action without consuming a spell slot twice, and you regain all expended uses of this ability when you finish a Long Rest. You can also cast this spell as a Bonus Action by expending a use of your Hemocraft. When you cast this on a creature, you also learn any Immunities, Resistances, and Vulnerabilities it has, as well as its creature type.

### Dark Augmentation (Level 10)

The magic of [[Atraphothoromancia|Atraphothoromancy]] suffuses your body to permanently reinforce your resilience.

Your speed increases by 10 feet, and you have a bonus to Strength, Dexterity, and Constitution saving throws equal to your Hemocraft modifier (minimum +1).

### Improved Brand of Castigation (Level 13)

The psychic damage from your Brand of Castigation increases to twice your Hemocraft modifier (minimum of 2) and the branded creature now suffers the damage from the brand if it deals damage to you or a creature you can see within 30 feet of you. 

Additionally, you can choose one of the following effects, which apply to a branded creature

***Corruption.*** Whenever the branded creature would regain hit points in any way other than taking a Short or Long Rest, it takes 2d6 necrotic damage.

***Tethering.*** The branded creature can't take the Dash action, and if it attempts to teleport or to leave its current plane by any means, it takes 4d6 psychic damage and must make a Wisdom saving throw. On a failure, the attempt to teleport or leave the plane fails.
 
### Sanguine Arcanum (Level 13)

Your mastery of blood magic allows you to channel a more powerful spell. Choose one level 4 spell from the Blood Hunter spell list. You always have that spell prepared, and it doesn't count against the number of spells you can prepare. Whenever you finish a Long Rest, you can replace the chosen spell with another level 4 spell from the Blood Hunter spell list.

You can cast the chosen spell once without expending a spell slot, and you regain the ability to do so when you finish a Short or Long Rest.

### Hardened Soul (Level 14)

Your will resists manipulation. You are immune to being [[Conditions#Charmed|charmed]] or [[Conditions#Frightened|frightened]].

### Cursed Brand (Level 17)

Your searing brand and blood curses work as one. Once per brand, when you invoke a blood curse on a creature branded by your Brand of Castigation, you can amplify the blood curse without losing hit points. You still gain the temporary hit points you would've gained from losing hit points when you amplify a blood curse this way.

Additionally, when you amplify a blood curse, its range is doubled.

### Epic Boon (Level 19)

You gain an [[Feats#Epic Boon Feats|Epic Boon feat]] or an other feat of your choice for which you qualify. 

### Sanguine Mastery (Level 20)

Your mastery of [[Atraphothoromancia]] reaches its height, mitigating your sacrifice and empowering your expertise. Once per turn, whenever a Blood Hunter feature requires you to roll a hemocraft die, you can roll the die twice and use either roll.

Additionally, you regain all expended uses of your Brand of Castigation when you finish a Short Rest.

### Blood Curse Options

The following options are available to your Hemocraft feature. the options are presented in alphabetical order.

#### Blood Curse of the Anxious
As a Bonus Action, you harry the body or mind of a creature within 30 feet of you, which must make a Wisdom saving throw. On a failed save, the cursed creature is Frightened until the end of your next turn.

***Amplify.*** The next Wisdom saving throw the cursed creature makes before this curse ends has disadvantage.

#### Blood Curse of Binding

As a Bonus Action, you attempt to bind a Large or smaller creature you can see within 30 feet of you, which must make a Strength saving throw. On a failure, the cursed creature's speed is reduced to 0 and it can't use Reactions until the end of your next turn.

***Amplify.*** On a failed save, this curse lasts for 1 minute and can affect any creature regardless of size. The cursed creature can repeat the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the curse on itself on a success. On a successful save, the target's speed is instead halved until the end of your next turn.

#### Blood Curse of Bloated Agony
As a Bonus Action, you curse a creature that you can see within 30 feet of you, causing its body to swell until the end of your next turn. For the duration, the creature has disadvantage on Strength checks and Dexterity checks, and takes Necrotic damage equal to one roll of your hemocraft die each time it makes more than one attack during its turn.

***Amplify.*** This curse lasts for 1 minute. The cursed creature can make a Constitution saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the curse on itself on a success.

#### Blood Curse of Exposure
When a creature you can see within 30 feet of you takes damage from an attack or spell, you can take a Reaction to temporarily weaken its resilience. Until the end of the target's next turn, it loses Resistance to all the damage types dealt by the triggering attack or spell (including for that triggering effect).

***Amplify.*** The target instead loses Immunity to the damage types of the triggering attack or spell, but has Resistance to those damage types until the end of its next turn.

#### Blood Curse of the Eyeless
When a creature you can see within 30 feet of you makes an attack, you can use your reaction to roll one hemocraft die and subtract the number rolled from the creature’s attack roll, potentially turning the success into a failure. The creature is immune to this curse if it is immune to the Blinded condition.

**_Amplify._** You apply this curse to all the creature’s attack rolls until the end of the creature’s turn. You roll separately for each affected attack.

#### Blood Curse of the Fallen Puppet
When a creature you can see within 30 feet of you drops to 0 hit points, you can take a Reaction to instill that creature with a final act of aggression. The creature immediately makes one weapon attack against a target of your choice within its range.

***Amplify.*** You can first cause the cursed creature to move up to half its speed, and you grant a bonus to its attack roll equal to your Hemocraft modifier (minimum of +1).

#### Blood Curse of the Marked
As a Bonus Action, you mark a creature that you can see within 30 feet of you. Until the start of your next turn, whenever the cursed creature takes damage from you of the same type as one of your active crimson rites, it takes an additional hemocraft die of damage.

***Amplify.*** The cursed creature takes the additional damage when it takes damage from any source, not just you, of the same type as one of your active crimson rites.

#### Blood Curse of the Muddled Mind
As a Bonus Action, you curse a creature that you can see within 30 feet of you that is concentrating on a spell or using a feature that requires concentration. That creature has disadvantage on the next Constitution saving throw it makes to maintain Concentration before the end of your next turn.

***Amplify***. The cursed creature has disadvantage on all Constitution saving throws made to maintain Concentration until the end of your next turn.

#### Blood Curse of Mutual Suffering

When you or an allied creature you can see within 30 feet of you takes damage, you can take a Reaction to link their pain to another. Choose a different creature you can see within 30 feet of you. The target of the curse receives half the damage received by the attacked creature as Psychic damage or the damage type of one of your active crimson rites.

***Amplify.*** This curse instead deals damage equal to the damage received by the attacked creature, and it trats damage immunities as resistances.

#### Blood Curse of the Reckless

As a Bonus Action, you make a creature you can see within 30 feet of you to become frenzied, unable to protect themselves. The target cannot take the Disengage or Dodge action until the end of your next turn.

***Amplify.*** The cursed creature's AC is reduced by 2 for the duration.

## Blood Hunter Subclasses

A Blood Hunter subclass is a specialization that grants you features at certain levels, as specified in the subclass.

A handful of secretive orders shape and define the knowledge of the blood hunters, their members all guarding unique arrays of cryptic techniques and rituals. Characters must seek out one of these orders to even be granted access to the Hunter’s Bane rite that starts each blood hunter’s journey. But only once a blood hunter has proven their dedication and worth will an order’s most powerful secrets be revealed.

### Order of the Ghostslayer

Ghostslayers seek out and study the moment of death, obsessing over the mystery of the transition from life, and the unholy power that can cause the dead to rise once more. These zealous blood hunters make it their life’s work to destroy the scourge of undeath wherever it is found, tuning their abilities to engage undead creatures and those who manipulate the necromancy that creates them.

#### Rite of the Dawn (Level 3)

You learn the Rite of the Dawn as part of your Crimson Rite feature. When you activate the Rite of the Dawn, the extra damage dealt by your rite is radiant damage. Additionally, while that rite is active on your weapon, you gain the following benefits:

- Your weapon sheds Bright Light out to a range of 20 feet.
- You have Resistance to Necrotic damage.
- When you hit an Undead creature with a weapon for which the Rite of the Dawn is active, you roll an additional hemocraft die when determining the extra damage from the rite.

#### Curse Specialist (Level 3)

You can invoke two blood curses with a single Bonus Action or Reaction. Each blood curse still consumes a separate use of your Hemocraft feature.

#### Aether Walk (Level 7)

At the start of your turn, you can step into the veil between the planes. For 1 minute, you can move through other creatures and objects as if they were difficult terrain, as well as see and affect creatures and objects on the Ethereal Plane. You take 1d10 force damage if you end your turn inside an object. If you are inside an object when this feature ends, you are immediately shunted to the nearest unoccupied space and you take force damage equal to twice the number of feet you moved.

You can use this feature twice. You regain one of its expended uses when you finish a Short Rest, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a Long Rest. You gain another use when you reach Blood Hunter level 15.

#### Brand of Sundering (Level 11)

Whenever you hit a branded creature with a weapon for which you have an active crimson rite, you roll an additional hemocraft die when determining the extra damage from the rite. Additionally, if a branded creature has the Incorporeal Movement trait or a similar feature, it can't move through creatures or objects while branded.

#### Blood Curse of the Exorcist (Level 15)

You hone your [[Atraphothoromancia|Atraphothoromancy]] to tear corruption from the minds and bodies of your allies —and to punish those responsible for it. You gain the **Blood Curse of the Exorcist**This doesn’t count against your number of blood curses known.

##### Blood Curse of the Exorcist

As a Bonus Action, you can choose one creature you can see within 30 feet of you that is Charmed, Frightened, or possessed. The target creature is no longer Charmed, Frightened, or possessed.

***Amplify.*** The creature that Charmed, Frightened, or possessed the target of your curse suffers 3d6 psychic damage and must make a Wisdom saving throw or be stunned until the end of your next turn.

#### Rite Revival (Level 18)

You learn to protect your fading life by reabsorbing the energy you feed to your weapons. If you have one or more crimson rites active and you are reduced to 0 hit points but don't die outright, you can choose to have all your active crimson rites end and drop to 1 hit point instead.

### Order of the Lycan

The ancient curse of lycanthropy is feared by nearly all peoples and cultures, passed through blood and seeding a host with the savage strength and hunger for violence of a wicked beast. The Order of the Lycan is a proud group of blood hunters who undergo “the Taming” —the ceremonial infliction of lycanthropy by a senior member of the order, for those who do not already carry the curse before seeking this path. These hunters then use the magic of their blood to harness the power of the monster they harbor, without losing themselves to it. Using intense will and secret blood magic rituals, members of the Order of the Lycan learn to control and unleash their hybrid forms for short periods of time. Enhanced physical prowess, unnatural resilience, and razor-sharp claws make these warriors a terrible foe to any evil that crosses their path. Yet no training is perfect, and without care and complete focus, even the greatest of blood hunters can temporarily lose themselves to their own hunger.

#### Heightened Senses (Level 3)

You gain the improved senses of a natural predator. You have advantage on [[Abilities#Perception|perception]] checks that rely on hearing or smell.

#### Hybrid Transformation (Level 3)

You learn to control the lycanthropic curse that courses through your veins. As a bonus action, you transform into a special hybrid form for up to 1 hour. You can speak, use equipment, and wear armor while in this form, and can revert to your normal form as a bonus action. You automatically revert to your normal form if you fall unconscious or die.

Once you use this feature, you can't do so again until you finish a Short or Long Rest. 

While you are transformed, you gain the following features:

**Feral Might.** You have advantage on Strength checks and Strength saving throws, and you have a +1 bonus to melee damage rolls. This bonus increases by 1 when you reach Blood Hunter level 11 (+2) and 18 (+3).

**Resilient Hide.** You have resistance to bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing damage not made with silvered weapons. Additionally, while you are not wearing heavy armor, you have a +1 bonus to AC.

**Predatory Strikes.** You can apply your Crimson Rite feature to your Unarmed Strikes, which you treat as a weapon. You can use Dexterity instead of Strength for the attack and damage rolls of your unarmed strikes, which deal 1d6 bludgeoning or slashing damage (your choice). This damage increases to 1d8 at Blood Hunter level 11. When you use the Attack action to make an Unarmed Strike, you can make one additional Unarmed Strike as part of that action.

**Bloodlust.** If you start your turn Bloodied, you must succeed on a DC 8 Wisdom saving throw or move directly toward the nearest creature and use the Attack action against that creature. If you're concentrating on a spell or are under an effect that prevents you from concentrating (such as the barbarian's Rage feature), you automatically fail this saving throw.

If you have your Extra Attack feature, you can choose whether to use it for this frenzied attack. If more than one creature is equally near to you, roll randomly to determine your target. Once your attack is resolved, you regain control of yourself.

**Lycan Regeneration** At the start of each of your turns when you have at least 1 hit point but fewer hit points than half your hit point maximum, you gain hit points equal to 1 + your Constitution modifier (minimum of 1). You gain these hit points before you must make the saving throw for your bloodlust.

#### Stalker's Prowess (Level 7)

The lycanthropy in you strengthens your body, granting you the following benefits.

**Enhanced Musculature.** Your speed is increased by 10 feet, and you add 10 feet to your long jump distance and 3 feet to your high jump distance.

**Improved Predatory Strikes.** While in your hybrid form, you have a +1 bonus to attack rolls made with your unarmed strike. This bonus increases by 1 when you reach Blood Hunter level 11 (+2) and 18 (+3).

#### Advanced Transformation (Level 11)

You learn to unleash and control more of the beast within. You can use your Hybrid Transformation feature twice, regaining all expended uses when you finish a short or long rest.

#### Brand of the Voracious (Level 15)

You have advantage on the saving throw for your bloodlust while in hybrid form. Additionally, your [[Blood Hunter#Brand of Castigation (Level 6)|Brand of Castigation]] can now bind a foe to your hunter’s ferocity. While in your hybrid form, you have advantage on attack rolls against a creature branded by you.

#### Rite Revival (Level 18)

You have mastered your inner predator. You can use your Hybrid Transformation feature an unlimited number of times, and your hybrid form lasts until you revert to your normal form, fall unconscious, or die.

### Order of the Mutant

The process of undertaking the Hunter’s Bane ritual is a painful, scarring, and sometimes fatal experience. Those who survive are irrevocably changed—and not always for the better. Over generations of experimentation, a splinter order of blood hunters honed the way in which [[Atraphothoromancia|Atraphothoromancy]] alters the body, using corrupted alchemy and toxic elixirs to alter their blood even further. Over time, they have modified their capabilities in battle, becoming something beyond what they once were. Calling themselves the Order of the Mutant, these blood hunters now specialize in assessing the strengths and weaknesses of their foes, altering their biology to be best prepared for any conflict.

#### Mutagencraft (Level 3)

You learn to master forbidden alchemical formulas —known as mutagens— that can temporarily alter your mental and physical abilities.

***Formulae.*** You learn 4 mutagen formulae from the Mutagen Options list. You learn one additional formulae at Blood Hunter level 7, 11, 15, and 18. When you learn a new mutagen formula, you can replace one formula you already know with a new mutagen formula.

***Mutagens.*** You prepare mutagens using your Alchemist's Supplies for various situations. When you finish a Long Rest, you can concoct 3 mutagens from the formulae you know with your Alchemist's Supplies. You can concoct one additional mutagen after a Long Rest when you reach Blood Hunter Level 11 (concoct 4 mutagens) and 18 (concoct 5 mutagens). Each mutagen must be a different one, you can't concoct the same mutagen multiple times.

When you finish a Short Rest, you can concoct one mutagen from the formulae you know with your Alchemist's Supplies. This mutagen must not be one you have currently concocted, though it can be one you have already consumed.

***Consuming Mutagens.*** As a Bonus Action, you can consume a mutagen you have concocted, gaining the effects of the mutagen, which lasts until you finish a Short or Long Rest. You can have one mutagen affecting you at a time. You can have an additional mutagen affecting you at one time when you reach Blood Hunter level 7 (two mutagens) and 15 (three mutagens). When you consume another mutagen past your limit, you choose a mutagen to purge, and it no longer affects you.

Mutagens are concocted to your biology, and have no effect on other creatures. Mutagens you have concocted are unstable by nature, and become inert when you finish a Long Rest.

##### Mutagens

The mutagens are presented here in alphabetical order. If a mutagen has a level written beside it, you must be an Order of the Mutant Blood Hunter of that level to choose that mutagen.

***Aether (Level 11).*** You gain a Flying Speed of 30 feet for 1 hour. 

***Alluring (Level 7).*** You have advantage on Charisma checks. Additionally, you are under the effect of the [[Alter Self]] spell, taking only the Change Appearance option, for 1 hour. 

***Celerity.*** Your Dexterity increases by 3, and so does your maximum. This increases to 4 at Blood Hunter level 15.

***Conversant (Level 7).*** You have advantage on Intelligence checks, and are under the effect of the [[Tongues]] spell.

***Cruelty (Level 11).*** When you take the Attack action on your turn, you can make an additional attack as a Bonus Action.

***Echolocation (Level 11).*** You have Blindsight up to 30 feet, or your Blindsight is increased by 30 feet. This Blindsight doesn't function if you are Deafened.

***Embers.*** You have Resistance to Fire damage.

***Gelid.*** You have Resistance to Cold damage.

***Impermeable.*** You have Resistance to Piercing damage.

***Mobile.*** You have Immunity to being Grappled or Restrained. At Blood Hunter level 11, you also have Immunity to being Paralyzed.

***Nighteye.*** You have Darkvision out to a range of 60 feet, or your Darkvision is increased by 60 feet.

***Percipient.*** You have advantage on Wisdom checks.

***Potency.*** Your Strength increases by 3, and so does your maximum. This increases to 4 at Blood Hunter level 15.

***Precision.*** Gain CRT(2). At Blood Hunter level 15, instead gain CRT(3).

***Rapidity.*** Your speed increases by 20 feet. At Blood Hunter level 15, it increases by an additional 5 feet.

***Reagent (Level 11).*** When you consume this mutagen, you can choose a mutagen you have concocted. This mutagen gains the effect of that mutagen. You can't concoct this mutagen again until you finish a Long Rest.

***Reconstruction (Level 7).*** You begin to rapidly heal. At the start of each of your turns, you regain hit points equal to your Blood Hunter level for 1 minute.

***Sagacity.*** Your Intelligence increases by 3, and so does your maximum. This increases to 4 at Blood Hunter level 15.

***Shielded.*** You have Resistance to Slashing damage.

***Unbreakable.*** You have Resistance to Bludgeoning damage.

***Vermillion.*** You gain an additional use of your Hemocraft.

#### Strange Metabolism (Level 7)

Your body begins to adapt to toxins and venoms, ignoring their corrupting effects. You gain immunity to poison damage and the [[Conditions#Poisoned|poisoned]] condition.

#### Brand of Axiom (Level 11)

Your mutagenic [[Atraphothoromancia|Atraphothoromancy]] lets your [[Blood Hunter#Brand of Castigation (Level 6)|Brand of Castigation]] reveal a foe’s true nature.

Any illusion or invisibility in effect on a creature when you brand it ends, and the creature can't benefit from invisibility or illusion effects while branded by you. If a creature branded by you is in an alternative form (such as through the [[Polymorph]] spell, the Change Shape action, Shapechanger trait, the Wild Shape feature, and similar effects) or it attempts to alter its form, it must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw or revert to its true form and be Stunned until the end of your next turn. 

#### Blood Curse of Corrosion (Level 15)

Your blood curse can infuse a creature’s body with terrible toxins.

As a Bonus Action, you cause a creature within 30 feet of you to become Poisoned. The cursed creature can make a Constitution saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the curse on itself on a success.

***Amplify.*** The cursed creature takes 4d6 necrotic damage when you inflict this curse, and it takes this damage again each time it fails a Constitution saving throw to end the curse.

#### Exalted Mutation (Level 18)

Your body can produce mutagens at a whim. At the start of your turn, choose one mutagen currently affecting you. Its effects end, and you can immediately have a mutagen you know the formula for take effect in its place.

You can use this feature a number of times equal to your Hemocraft modifier (minimum of once). You regain all expended uses when you finish a Long Rest.

### Order of the Profane Soul

The Profane Soul draws magic from many different creatures. They do this in many different ways; some form pacts, some siphon energies, and some bind their souls with them. Otherworldly threats require otherworldly power to deal with.

#### Otherwordly Attunement (Level 3)

You draw magic from certain kinds of creatures, which fuel your abilities. Choose a creature type from the following list. You gain a benefit related to that creature type. When you finish a Long Rest, you can choose to draw magic from a different creature type, and change your choice.

***Aberration.*** When you cast a spell with a Pact Magic spell slot, or you score a critical hit with a weapon with an active Crimson Rite, you can cause creatures of your choice within 10 feet of you to become Charmed by you until the end of your next turn. A creature affected by this effect is immune to it for 1 hour.

***Celestial.*** As a Bonus Action, you can expend a use of your Hemocraft and choose a creature within 60 feet of you. That creature regains a number of hit points equal to one roll of your hemocraft die plus your Hemocraft modifier (minimum of 1)

***Elemental.*** As a Bonus Action, you can expend a use of your Hemocraft and gain a Flying Speed of 30 feet. This speed lasts a number of rounds equal to your Hemocraft modifier (minimum of 1).

***Fey.*** When you damage a creature with your Crimson Rite, that creature glows with faint light until the end of your next turn. For the duration, the creature gains no benefit from any cover or from being invisible.

***Fiend.*** When you damage a creature with your Crimson Rite, you can reroll any 1 or 2 rolled as part of the attack's damage, and you must use the new roll.

***Undead.*** When you cast a spell with a Pact Magic spell slot, or you score a critical hit with a weapon with an active Crimson Rite, you can cause creatures of your choice within 10 feet of you to become Frightened by you until the end of your next turn. A creature affected by this effect is immune to it for 1 hour.

#### Pact Magic (Level 3)

You have learned to draw on otherworldly magic to cast spells.

**Cantrips.** You know two cantrips of your choice from the Warlock spell list. [[Eldritch Blast]] and [[Mind Sliver]] are recommended. Whenever you gain a Blood Hunter level, you can replace one cantrip you learned from this feature with another Warlock cantrip of your choice.

When you reach Blood Hunter level 10, you learn another Warlock cantrip of your choice, as shown in the Profane Soul Pact Magic table.

**Spell Slots.** The Profane Soul Pact Magic table shows how many Pact Magic spell slots you have to cast your level 1+ spells. The table also shows the level of those slots, all of which are the same level. You regain all expended Pact Magic slots when you finish a Short or Long Rest.

**Prepared Spells of Level 1+.** You prepare the list of level 1+ spells available for you to cast with this feature. To start, choose two level 1 spells from the Warlock spell list. [[Armor of Agathys]] and [[Hex]] are recommended.

The number of spells on your list increases as you gain Blood Hunter levels, as shown in the Prepared Spells column of the Profane Soul Pact Magic table. Whenever that number increases, choose additional Warlock spells until the number on your list matches the number in the table. Each chosen spell must be of a level no higher than the Slot Level shown in the table. For example, at Blood Hunter level 7, your list can include four Warlock spells of levels 1 and 2 in any combination. Spells prepared through other features, including Blood Magic, Sanguine Arcanum, Otherworldly Arcana, and Eldritch Arcana, don't count against this number.

**Changing Your Prepared Spells.** Whenever you gain a Blood Hunter level, you can replace one spell prepared through this feature with another Warlock spell of a level no higher than the Slot Level shown in the table.

**Spellcasting Ability.** Your Hemocraft modifier is your spellcasting ability for spells learned or prepared through this feature.

**Spellcasting Focus.** You can use an Arcane Focus or a weapon with an active Crimson Rite as a Spellcasting Focus.

> **Campaign Integration.** While you have this Pact Magic feature, Non-Natural Spellcasting doesn't grant you separate cantrips, prepared spells, or spell slots. You retain the additional cantrips and always-prepared spells granted independently by Blood Magic and Sanguine Arcanum, as well as benefits from other features. You can select eligible generic cantrips and spells through Pact Magic in place of choices from the Warlock list. They count against this feature's normal allowances and follow its level and replacement rules, as well as the generic list's resonance requirements. A generic spell chosen through this Blood Hunter feature counts as a Blood Hunter spell for you. If it is also on the Warlock list, you can choose it from that list instead, in which case it counts as a Warlock spell for you.

#### Blood Curse of the Souleater (Level 18)

You can expend on use of your Hemocraft to invoke a special blood curse. 

When a creature that isn't a construct or undead is reduced to 0 hit points within 30 feet of you, you can take a Reaction to absorb their life energy and empower yourself. Until the end of your next turn, you make attacks with advantage and you have resistance to all damage types.

***Amplify.*** Additionally, you regain an expended Pact Magic spell slot. Once you've amplified this blood curse, you must finish a Long Rest before you can amplify it again.
