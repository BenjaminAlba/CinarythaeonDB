# Magus
Magi are gifted warriors that blend martial prowess with mystical puissance. Wielding spell and steel in tandem, Magi can render their enemies vulnerable to their magic with a look and invoke sigils to enhance their offensive and defensive abilities. Leveraging these diverse strengths, Magi can overcome even the most powerful adversaries and challenging obstacles.

Every Magi belongs to an esoteric order that shapes their abilities, enhances their sigils, and expands their magical capabilities. These orders also inform or reflect a Magus' goals and reasons for adventuring. Some orders compel Magi to catalogue the magical wonders of the multiverse while others call on Magi to deliver harsh and unrepentant justice. Just as a Magus balances their martial talents with their magical abilities, they must also balance their personal aspirations with their order's goals.

## Class Features

### Esoteric Eye (Level 1)

As a Bonus Action, you can observe a creature you can see. Until the end of your next turn, you have Advantage on your Magus spell attacks against it and it has Disadvantage on saving throws against your Magus spells.

You can use this Bonus Action a number of times equal to your Intelligence modifier (minimum once). You regain one expended use when you finish a Short Rest, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a Long Rest.

### Spellcasting (Level 1)

With disciplined rehearsal, you can wage war and cast spells by rote. See the core rules for the rules on spellcasting. The information below details how you use those rules with Magus spells, which appear in the Magus spell list later in the class's description.

**Cantrips.** You know two Magus cantrips of your choice. [[Chill Touch]] and [[Fire Bolt]] are recommended. Whenever you gain a Magus level, you can replace one of your cantrips from this feature with another Magus cantrip of your choice.

When you reach Magus levels 10 and 14, you learn another Magus cantrip of your choice, as shown in the Cantrips column of the Magus Features table.

**Spell Slots.** The Magus Features table shows how many spell slots you have to cast your level 1+ spells. You regain all expended spell slots when you finish a Long Rest.

Prepared Spells of Level 1+. You prepare the list of level 1+ spells that are available for you to cast with this feature. To start, choose two level 1 Magus spells. Chromatic Orb and Shield are recommended.

The number of spells on your list increases as you gain Magus levels, as shown in the Prepared Spells column of the Magus Features table. Whenever that number increases, choose additional Magus spells until the number of spells on your list matches the number in the Magus Features table. The chosen spells must be of a level for which you have spell slots. For example, if you're a level 5 Magus, your list of prepared spells can include six Magus spells of level 1 or 2 in any combination.

If another Magus feature gives you spells that you always have prepared, those spells don't count against the number of spells you can prepare with this feature, but those spells otherwise count as Magus spells for you.

**Changing Your Prepared Spells.** Whenever you gain a Magus level, you can replace one spell on your list with another Magus spell for which you have spell slots.

**Spellcasting Ability.** Intelligence is your spellcasting ability for your Magus spells.

### Weapon Mastery (Level 1)

Your training with weapons allows you to use the mastery properties of two kinds of weapons of your choice with which you have proficiency, such as Rapiers and Scimitars.

Whenever you finish a Long Rest, you can change the kinds of weapons you chose. For example, you could switch to using the mastery properties of Longbows and Longswords.

### Fighting Style (Level 2)

You gain a Fighting Style feat of your choice. Instead of choosing one of those feats, you can choose the option below.

***Mystic Warrior - Rebalanced.*** When you make a Constitution saving throw to maintain Concentration, you can gain a bonus to the saving throw equal to your Intelligence modifier (minimum of +1). If you fail the saving throw, you gain Temporary Hit Points equal to twice the spell's level plus your Intelligence modifier. You can use this feature a number of times equal to your Intelligence modifier (minimum of once), and you regain all expended uses when you finish a Long Rest.

### Mystic Mark (Level 2)

You can take a Bonus Action to imbue yourself with magical power and resilience. When you do, choose a Magus level 1+ spell you have prepared that has a casting time of an action or a Bonus Action, expend a spell slot equal to or higher than the spell level, and choose Force damage or a damage type dealt by the spell. You gain your choice of the Aegis or Enspell benefit.

**Aegis.** You have Resistance to the chosen damage type.

**Enspell.** When you deal damage with a weapon or Unarmed Strike, you can deal the chosen damage type instead of the normal damage type for the attack.

You can only benefit from one Aegis and one Enspell at a time. While you have an Aegis or Enspell, you can use a Bonus Action to release and cast the spell chosen using this feature. Your Mystic Mark and its corresponding benefit end after 1 hour, or when you release the spell.

### Magus Subclass (Level 3)

You gain a Magus subclass of your choice. A subclass is a specialization that grants you features at certain Magus levels. For the rest of your career, you gain each of your subclass's features that are of your Magus level or lower.

### Ability Score Improvement (Level 4)

You gain the Ability Score Improvement feat or another feat of your choice for which you qualify. You gain this feature again at Magus levels 8, 12, and 16.

### Arcane Ability - Rebalanced (Level 5)

When you fail an ability check, you can imbue your effort with magical might. You can expend a spell slot (no action required) to add a bonus to the check equal to the level of the expended spell slot plus your Intelligence modifier, potentially turning the failure into a success.

Once you use this feature, you can't do so again until you finish a Short or Long Rest unless you expend a spell slot (no action required) to restore your use of it.

### Sword and Sorcery (Level 5)

When you take the Attack action and it doesn't benefit from the Extra Attack feature, you can cast a Magus cantrip with a casting time of an action as part of the same action. When you take the Magic action on your turn, you can use a Bonus Action to make one weapon attack.

### Magical Recovery (Level 6)

You can regain some of your magical energy by meditating on the Tapestry and its myriad threads. When you finish a Short Rest, you can choose expended spell slots to recover. The spell slots can have a combined level equal to no more than half your Magus level (round up), and none of the slots can be level 6 or higher. For example, if you're a level 6 Magus, you can recover up to three levels' worth of spell slots, regaining either one level 3 spell slot, a level 2 and a level 1 spell slot, or three level 1 spell slots.

Once you use this feature, you can't do so again until you finish a Long Rest.

### Rite of Return (Level 9)

When your Mystic Mark ends without you releasing the spell, you regain the expended spell slot. Additionally, you can end your Mystic Mark as a Bonus Action, regaining the expended spell slot.

### All-Seeing Eye (Level 10)

As a Bonus Action you can expend a use of your Esoteric Eye to gain Truesight out to a distance of 60 feet for 10 minutes. During this time, you can use the Bonus Action granted by your Esoteric Eye without requiring or expending any uses.

Once you use this feature, you can't use it again until you finish a Long Rest.

### Sigil Surge (Level 11)

The benefits of your Mystic Mark are improved.

***Aegis.*** At the start of each of your turns, you gain Temporary Hit Points equal to the expended spell slot level plus your Intelligence modifier.

***Enspell.*** When you deal damage with a weapon or Unarmed Strike, you deal an additional 1d6 damage of the chosen type.

### Sorcerous Shielding (Level 15)

You gain the following benefits.

***Magic Resistance.*** Choose one of the following damage types: Force, Necrotic, Psychic, or Radiant. You have Resistance to the chosen damage type. You can change your choice whenever you finish a Long Rest.

***Spell Parry.*** When you make a saving throw against a spell or a spell attack roll is made against you, you can take a Reaction to gain Advantage on the saving throw or impose Disadvantage on the attack roll. You must use this Reaction before the roll is made.

### Spell Surge (Level 18)

In times of need you can surge with arcane power, grasping at magic typically beyond your ability. Choose one level 6 Magus spell as your surge spell. You can cast your surge spell once without expending a spell slot. When you do, you regain one spell slot of your choice that is 5th level or lower.

Once you cast your surge spell you must finish a Long Rest before you can cast it in this way again.

### Epic Boon (Level 19)

You gain an Epic Boon feat or another feat of your choice for which you qualify. Boon of Spell Recall is recommended.

### Archmagus (Level 20)

You gain the following benefits.

**Ability Score Enhancement.** Choose Strength or Dexterity. Your Intelligence and the chosen ability score increase by 2, to a maximum of 23.

**Rapid Recovery.** You can use your Magical Recovery twice, regaining both uses when you finish a Long Rest.

## Magus Subclasses

A Magus subclass is a specialization that grants you features at certain levels, as specified in the subclass.

### Arcane Order
*Defend the Arcane Architecture of the World*

The Arcane Order seeks to catalog the magical wonders of Cinarythaeon and defend the Tapestry from existential threats. You travel the world with an eye out for lost artifacts, magical monuments, and mystic secrets. Although some in the Arcane Order collect such prizes in a quest for power, most do so out of intellectual curiosity or the desire to preserve such treasures for future generations.

#### Amethyst Mark (Level 3)

The effects of your mystic marks are enhanced by your greater understanding of the Arcane Order's esoteric knowledge. When you use your Mystic Mark feature, a faint purple, crystalline aura surrounds you and you gain the following effects based on the benefit or benefits chosen.

***Aegis.*** When a willing creature you can see within 30 feet of you is hit by an attack, you can use your Reaction to swap places via teleportation. You suffer the effects of the attack instead of the creature and you have Resistance to damage dealt by the attack.

***Enspell.*** Once per turn when you hit a creature with a weapon or Unarmed Strike, you can force it to make a Charisma saving throw against your spell save DC. On a failure, the creature is teleported 10 feet horizontally in a direction of your choosing.

#### Arcane Order Magic (Level 3)

You can use an Arcane Focus as a Spellcasting Focus for your Magus spells.

In addition, your training in the Arcane Order ensures you always have certain spells ready; when you reach a Magus level specified in the Arcane Order Spells table, you thereafter always have the listed spells prepared.

#### Mystic Insights (Level 3)

You gain the following benefits.

***Additional Cantrips.*** You know two cantrips of your choice from the Wizard spell list. These cantrips count as Magus cantrips for you but don't count against your number of cantrips known.

***Arcana Adept.*** You gain proficiency in the Arcana skill. If you are already proficient with Arcana, you gain Expertise in it instead.

#### Lesser Spell Surge (Level 7)

You can cast a Magus spell you have prepared without expending a spell slot, as if you had expended your highest level spell slot. Once you do, you cannot do so again until you finish a Long Rest.

#### Occult Savant (Level 7)

Choose two spells of a level you can cast from the Wizard spell list. You always have the chosen spells prepared. Whenever you gain a Magus level, you can replace one of the chosen spells with another spell from the Wizard spell list of a level you can cast.

#### Arcane Vortex (Level 14)

As a Magic action, choose a point you can see within 60 feet of yourself, warping space in a 20-foot radius Sphere around it. You and your allies within the area can each teleport up to 30 feet in a direction of your choice. All other creatures in the area must make a Strength saving throw against your spell save DC. On a failed save, a creature takes 6d8 Force damage, is pulled up to 15 feet closer to the point, and can't use its Speed to move away from that point until the start of your next turn. On a successful save, a creature takes half as much damage only.

Once you use this feature, you can't use it again until you finish a Long Rest.

#### Master of the Arcane (Level 20)

You gain the following benefits.

***Amethyst Spell Surge.*** You can expend the use of your Spell Surge to cast [[Chain Lightning]] or [[Contingency]].

***Vexing Vortices.*** When you finish a Short Rest, you can regain the use of your Arcane Vortex. Once you regain the use of it in this way, you can't do so again until you finish a Long Rest.

Legend has it that in a time before recorded history, an elite host of heroic travelers forsook their immortality to protect the people and places of the Material Plane. The story goes that these martyrs' legacy lives on in the Heaven's Guard, an order of Magi that act as bodyguards to the righteous and sentinels of hallowed temples. Whether or not such tales are true, Magi in the Heaven's Guard eagerly serve as living shields to their chosen charges by drawing their enemies' ire and healing what wounds their adversaries manage to inflict.

***Aegis.*** When a willing creature you can see within 30 feet of you is hit by an attack, you can use your Reaction to guard the creature until the start of your next turn. While the creature is guarded this way, attacks against it have Disadvantage.

***Enspell.*** Once per turn when you hit a creature with a weapon or Unarmed Strike, it has Disadvantage on attacks against creatures other than you until the start of your next turn.

#### Heaven's Guard Magic (Level 3)

You can use a Holy Symbol as a Spellcasting Focus for your Magus spells.

In addition, your training in the Heaven's Guard ensures you always have certain spells ready; when you reach a Magus level specified in the Heaven's Guard Spells table, you thereafter always have the listed spells prepared.

*Haunt the Unrighteous*

These Magi believe that the means justify the ends, channeling dark magic to avenge injustices and bring evil to heel.

#### Obsidian Mark - Rebalanced (Level 3)

The effects of your mystic marks are enhanced by your greater understanding of the Knights Stygian's esoteric knowledge. ***Enspell.*** Once per turn when you hit a creature with an attack with a weapon or Unarmed Strike, you can deal an additional 1d4 Necrotic damage. If the target is Bloodied, the additional damage increases to 2d4.

#### Doombringer (Level 3)

***Reaper's Reward.*** Once per turn when you hit a creature with a weapon or Unarmed Strike, you can expend and roll a Hit Point Die. You regain Hit Points, and deal additional Necrotic damage to the creature, equal to a roll of the Hit Point Die.

### Shadow Court
*Fight for Control from the Shadows*

Although outsiders see the Shadow Court as a singular group, it is in fact multiple clans that wage clandestine wars against one another to obtain secrets and access to resources. Magi in the Shadow Court specialize in legerdemain, stealth, and assassination. Using these tools, these Magi manipulate political powers to stymie their enemies and advance their own agendas.

#### Jade Mark - Rebalanced (Level 3)

The effects of your mystic marks are enhanced by your greater understanding of the Shadow Court's esoteric knowledge. When you use your Mystic Mark feature, you are engulfed in an aura of translucent smoke and you gain the following effects based on the benefit or benefits chosen.

***Aegis.*** When a creature you can see within 30 feet of you is hit by an attack, you can use your Reaction to give the creature the Invisible condition until the end of its next turn. The condition ends early if the target makes an attack roll, deals damage, or casts a spell.

***Enspell.*** Once per turn when you hit a creature with a weapon or Unarmed Strike, you can make the target overexposed until the start of your next turn or until it is hit with a Critical Hit. While overexposed, attack rolls against the target can benefit from an additional CRT(1).

#### Hand and Foot Training (Level 3)
You gain the following benefits.

***Covert Warrior.*** You gain proficiency in the Stealth skill and with Thieves' Tools. If you are already proficient with Stealth, you gain Expertise in it instead.

***Enshadowed Equipment.*** You can take a Bonus Action to cause one of the following objects to magically coalesce from shadows in your hands: Caltrops, Costume, Crowbar, Grappling Hook, Ladder, Net, Parchment, Rope, Signal Whistle, Spyglass, or Thieves' Tools. This item remains until it has been exposed to Bright Light for 10 minutes, you dismiss it (no action required), or you use this Bonus Action again.

You can use this Bonus Action a number of times equal to your Intelligence modifier (minimum once), and you regain all expended uses when you finish a Long Rest.

***Martial Artist.*** You can use your Dexterity modifier instead of your Strength modifier for the attack and damage rolls of your Unarmed Strikes, and you can roll 1d6 in place of the normal damage of your Unarmed Strike. In addition, when you use the Grapple or Shove option of your Unarmed Strike, you can use your Dexterity modifier instead of your Strength modifier to determine the save DC.

#### Shadow Court Magic (Level 3)

You can use Parchment as a Spellcasting Focus for your Magus spells. When you do, if the spell is level 1+ the Parchment is destroyed.

In addition, your training in the Shadow Court ensures you always have certain spells ready; when you reach a Magus level specified in the Shadow Court Spells table, you thereafter always have the listed spells prepared.

When you cast [[Mirror Image]], each time a duplicate is destroyed the attacking creature must succeed on an Intelligence saving throw against your Magus spell save DC or take Psychic damage equal to your Magus level plus your Intelligence modifier.

Once you use this Bonus Action, you can't use it again until you finish a Long Rest.

#### False Body - Rebalanced (Level 14)

You can replace yourself with an unlikely body double in the instant before you take a blow. When you are hit by an attack, you can take a Reaction to reduce the damage from that attack to 1. When you do, you have the Invisible condition for 1 minute and teleport to an unoccupied space of your choice within 300 feet of you that you can see. You leave a block of wood or another harmless, mundane object in the space you teleported away from.

Once you use this feature, you can't use it again until you finish a Long Rest.

#### Enshadowed (Level 20)

You gain the following benefits.

***Jade Spell Surge.*** You can expend the use of your Spell Surge to cast [[Eyebite]] or [[Programmed Illusion]].

***Shadow Puppeteer.*** When you finish a Short Rest, you can regain the use of your False Body. Once you regain the use of it in this way, you can't do so again until you finish a Long Rest.

### Sidereal Cabal
*Master Your Destiny (and Everyone Else's Too)*

Magi in the Sidereal Cabal consult the stars to foresee the future and intercede at moments of particular import. Long ago the cabal followed a philosophy of balance, believing that destiny and free will should be given equal weight. Today the cabal is split into two camps, kismetists and volitionists. Kismetists prefer indirect and discreet methods, believing that active and obvious interference in the world's affairs cause a disruption in the natural order of fate. Volitionists find pivotal moments where the road of destiny forks and interject themselves to force the outcome they prefer.

#### Selenite Mark (Level 3)

The effects of your mystic marks are enhanced by your greater understanding of the Sidereal Cabal's esoteric knowledge. When you use your Mystic Mark feature, you give off a soft indistinct multicolored glow and hum with potential energy. You gain the following effects based on the benefit or benefits chosen.

***Aegis.*** When a willing creature you can see within 30 feet of you is hit by an attack, you can use your Reaction to make the attacker reroll the attack with Disadvantage. It must use the new result.

***Enspell.*** Once per turn when you miss a creature with a weapon or Unarmed Strike, you can make the attack roll again with Advantage. You must use the new result.

#### Starlit Sight - Rebalanced (Level 3)

You gain the following benefits.

***Darkvision.*** You have Darkvision with a range of 60 feet. If you already have Darkvision when you gain this feature, its range increases by 60 feet.

***Mini-Portent.*** When you finish a Long Rest, you can expend one of your Hit Point Dice, roll it, and record the number rolled. Before you or a creature you can see makes a D20 Test, you can add or subtract the recorded number from the roll. You can use this foretold number only once, and you lose it if you haven't used it when you finish your next Long Rest.

***Revelator's Eyes.*** You gain proficiency in the Perception skill. If you are already proficient with Perception, you gain Expertise in it instead.

#### Celestial Clockwork Control - Rebalanced (Level 7)

Your extensive study of the stars has taught you to twist the heavenly mechanics of fate in your favor. After a creature you can see within 60 feet of you makes a D20 Test you can expend a spell slot to modify the result of the roll. When you do, you can choose to increase or decrease the result of the roll by the level of the expended spell slot, potentially turning a failure into a success or vice versa.

Once you use this feature, you can't use it again until you finish a Short or Long Rest.

#### Seal Fate (Level 14)

When a creature that you can see within 60 feet makes a roll with Advantage or Disadvantage, you can seal its fate. The creature rolls three d20s, rather than two, using the highest result if it had Advantage or the lowest result if it had Disadvantage.

Once you use this feature, you can't use it again until you finish a Short Rest or Long Rest.

#### Starfinder (Level 20)

You gain the following benefits.

***Selenite Spell Surge.*** You can expend the use of your Spell Surge to cast [[First Speech]] or [[True Seeing]].

***Weave Destiny.*** You can use your Seal Fate three times, regaining one use when you finish a Short Rest and all uses when you finish a Long Rest.

### Sylvan Circle
*Protect Wild Creatures and Natural Wonders*

The Sylvan Circle acts as the sword and shield of the natural world. You adventure to ensure that the world's natural wonders are preserved and that the diversity of its flora and fauna are not threatened. Although some Sylvan Circle Magi become hardliners that see themselves at war with civilization, most work to strike a balance between the needs of the natural world and it's various peoples.

#### Emerald Mark (Level 3)

The effects of your mystic marks are enhanced by your greater understanding of the Sylvan Circle's esoteric knowledge. When you use your Mystic Mark feature, a verdant green aura surrounds you and you gain the following effects based on the benefit or benefits chosen.

***Aegis.*** When you or a willing creature you can see within 30 feet of you fails a saving throw, you can use your Reaction to give the roll a bonus equal to your Intelligence modifier (minimum +1), potentially turning the failure into a success.

***Enspell.*** Once per turn when you hit a creature with a weapon or Unarmed Strike, you can cause the target to treat all spaces as Difficult Terrain until the end of its next turn.

#### Agrestal Spirit (Level 3)

You gain the following benefits.

***Additional Cantrips.*** You know two additional cantrips of your choice from the Magus or Druid spell list. These cantrips count as Magus cantrips for you but don't count against your number of cantrips known.

***Green Hand.*** You gain proficiency in the Nature skill. If you are already proficient with Nature, you gain Expertise in it instead.

***Wild Instincts.*** You can take the Disengage action as a Bonus Action. When you do, until the end of your turn your Speed increases by 15 feet and you have Advantage on Strength (Athletics) and Dexterity (Acrobatics) checks.

You can use this Bonus Action a number of times equal to your Intelligence modifier (minimum once), and you regain all expended uses when you finish a Long Rest.

#### Sylvan Circle Spells (Level 3)

You can use a Druidic Focus as a Spellcasting Focus for your Magus spells.

In addition, your training in the Sylvan Circle ensures you always have certain spells ready; when you reach a Magus level specified in the Sylvan Circle Spells table, you thereafter always have the listed spells prepared.

#### Nature's Mask (Level 7)

You always have the [[Polymorph]] spell prepared. You can cast the spell on yourself without expending a spell slot, when you do, the spell does not require Concentration and its duration changes to 8 hours. You can end the spell at any point prior to the end of its duration (no action required).

Once you cast the spell in this way, you can't do so again until you finish a Short Rest or Long Rest.

#### Font of Life (Level 14)

As a Bonus Action, you can manifest sylvan energies in a 15-foot Emanation that originates from you for 1 minute. It ends early if you dismiss it (no action required) or have the Incapacitated condition.

When you manifest the Emanation and at the start of each of your subsequent turns, creatures of your choice within your Emanation regain Hit Points equal to 1d8 plus your Intelligence modifier (minimum of one). Additionally, creatures of your choice within your Emanation have Advantage on saving throws.

Once you use this feature, you must finish a Long Rest before you can do so again.

#### Sylvan Savant (Level 20)

You gain the following benefits.

***Emerald Spell Surge.*** You can expend the use of your Spell Surge to cast [[Conjure Fey]] or [[Wall of Thorns]].

***Font Overflowing.*** When you finish a Short Rest, you can regain the use of your Font of Life. Once you regain the use of it in this way, you can't do so again until you finish a Long Rest.
