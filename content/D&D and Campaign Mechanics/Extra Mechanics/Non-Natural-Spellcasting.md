# Non-Natural Spellcasting

Magic is available to every character in Cinarythaeon. The following feature grants spellcasting to characters whose class does not provide it. If you gain spellcasting from another class or subclass feature, combine your allowances as described under **Other Spellcasting Features**.

## Spellcasting

You have learned to channel magic through your own aptitude and resonances. You use the normal rules for casting spells, with the additions below and the campaign rules later in this document.

### Cantrips

You know one cantrip of your choice from the generic spell list. You must meet its resonance requirement, if any. You learn a second eligible cantrip at character level 3, as shown in the Non-Natural Spellcasting table.

Whenever you gain a character level, you can replace one cantrip you learned through this feature with another eligible cantrip from the generic list.

### Spell Slots

The Non-Natural Spellcasting table shows how many spell slots this feature provides. Your character level determines your progression in the table. You regain all expended slots provided by this feature when you finish a Long Rest.

### Prepared Spells of Level 1+

You prepare the list of level 1+ spells available to cast with this feature. To start, choose two level 1 spells from the generic spell list, meeting each spell's resonance requirement, if any.

The number of spells on your list increases as shown in the Prepared Spells column. Whenever that number increases, choose additional eligible generic spells until your list matches the number shown. Each spell must be of a level available to you through this feature.

For example, at character level 5, this feature allows four prepared spells of levels 1 and 2 in any combination.

***Changing Your Prepared Spells.*** Whenever you finish a Long Rest, you can replace one spell prepared through this feature with another eligible spell from the generic list.

### Spellcasting Ability

Choose Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma when you create your character. The chosen ability is your spellcasting ability for spells granted by this feature.

- **Spell save DC:** 8 + your Proficiency Bonus + your spellcasting ability modifier.
- **Spell attack modifier:** your Proficiency Bonus + your spellcasting ability modifier.

If you gain class or subclass spellcasting, use that feature's spellcasting ability for your generic spells instead. If you later gain spellcasting from another class, retain the ability established by the first class or subclass feature that replaced your original choice. Each class's own spells continue to use the ability specified by that class. This feature does not alter multiclass prerequisites.

## Other Spellcasting Features

If you have spellcasting from a class or subclass, compare its applicable allowances with those in the Non-Natural Spellcasting table. Use the higher allowance for each resource separately; do not add the generic allowance to the other allowance.

***Cantrips and Prepared Spells.*** Compare your cantrip allowance and your prepared-spell allowance separately. If the generic allowance is higher, fill the additional selections with eligible generic spells. These supplementary selections follow this feature's replacement rules. Selections provided by your class or subclass follow that feature's normal acquisition, preparation, and replacement rules.

***Spell Slots.*** Compare the number of slots at each spell level separately and use the higher number at that level. An absent entry counts as zero. For example, if generic casting grants two level 1 slots, two level 2 slots, and one level 3 slot, while your class grants four level 1 slots and three level 2 slots, you have four level 1 slots, three level 2 slots, and one level 3 slot.

***Spell-Level Access.*** A spell level available solely through generic casting grants access to only eligible generic spells of that level.

***Multiclassing.*** Determine your normal multiclass Spellcasting slots first, then compare that progression with generic casting at each slot level. Determine your class cantrips and prepared spells separately for each class, then compare their total allowances with the generic allowances. Any shortfall is filled by supplementary generic selections; you cannot transfer a class's preparations to another class. Generic casting does not add caster levels to the normal multiclass calculation.

***Pact Magic.*** Pact Magic slots remain separate and retain their own level, number, and recovery rules. Do not merge them into the slot-by-slot comparison for Spellcasting. A character with Pact Magic still receives any applicable generic Spellcasting allowance.

***Additional Spell Grants.*** Spells and cantrips granted by feats, species traits, and other additional features are not absorbed by this comparison. Likewise, spells a feature makes always prepared without counting against your allowance remain additional.

# Campaign Spellcasting Rules

The following rules apply to both natural and non-natural spellcasters.

## Generic Spell List

The generic spell list expands the spells available through your spellcasting features. It does not grant additional class spell slots, cantrips, or prepared spells beyond the allowances described above.

***Resonance Requirements.*** If a generic spell requires resonance, you must share at least one of its assigned magical affinities to select it through the generic list. Matching either affinity of a spell with two affinities is sufficient. A generic spell marked as not requiring resonance is available without a matching affinity.

***Class Spells.*** When you select a generic spell through a class or subclass spellcasting feature, it counts as a spell of that class for you. It uses that feature's spellcasting ability and follows its normal acquisition, spell-level, preparation, and replacement rules. For example, a Wizard acquiring a generic spell through Wizard spellcasting must still follow the rules for adding spells to their spellbook.

***Independent Access.*** If a spell is already available through your class list, subclass, feat, species trait, or another feature, you can acquire it through that source without meeting the generic list's resonance requirement. That source's other requirements still apply.

***Preparation Required.*** Resonance makes a spell eligible for selection; it does not automatically teach or prepare the spell. Generic spells selected through a feature count toward its normal allowance unless that feature states otherwise.

## Resonance Effects

Resonance effects function only during combat, and lasting resonance effects end when combat ends. The DM determines when combat begins and ends. Each effect uses the trigger and other rules in its resonance description.

***One Resonance per Casting.*** A spell you cast can activate effects of only one of its resonances that you possess. Choose which resonance to activate when its effect's trigger occurs, following that resonance's rules. Once you activate it, that casting can't activate a different resonance, including through its later effects. A missed trigger can't be used retroactively.

The chosen resonance can activate more than once from an ongoing spell if its own rules allow it. An effect from an earlier casting can still apply, even if it has a different resonance from the one chosen for the current casting. For example, existing Corruption can add damage to a later spell that activates a different resonance, subject to Corruption's rules.

An ongoing Nihilokathra spell cast before combat can apply Corruption when it deals damage during combat, provided it meets Corruption's requirements. A resonance effect triggered by casting a spell can't activate retroactively if the spell was cast before combat.

***Overlapping Effects.*** A creature can be affected by only one lasting effect of a given resonance from the same caster at a time. If that caster would apply another lasting effect of the same resonance to the creature, the caster chooses whether to keep the existing effect or replace it with the new one. Effects from different casters can affect the same creature at the same time and are tracked separately. This rule applies to allies, enemies, and the caster; it doesn't limit instantaneous effects or change a resonance's own frequency limits.

***Corruption Exception.*** Corruption continues to accumulate in one shared total, including stacks applied by the same caster or by different casters. Its stacks have no maximum, and its other rules apply normally.

***Oboromancia: Quick Handling.*** Quick Handling can be used whether or not the spell expends a spell slot, including when the spell is a cantrip or is cast through Single-Resonance Casting. Its other requirements apply normally.

## Single-Resonance Casting

If you have resonance with exactly one magic type, you can draw on it to cast a spell without expending a spell slot.

***Free Casting.*** Once per combat, you can cast a level 1–3 spell you have available to cast that shares your resonance without expending a spell slot. You can use this benefit a number of times equal to your highest spellcasting ability modifier (minimum of once), and you regain all expended uses when you finish a Long Rest. If you use more than one spellcasting ability, use the highest modifier among the abilities you use to cast spells.

***Casting Requirements.*** The free casting uses the spell's normal casting time, components, Concentration, and other requirements, and casts the spell at its lowest level. It counts toward that spell's casting limit unless the casting is exempt under the Warlock Exception below; otherwise, you must have a use of the spell remaining.

## Casting Limits

You can cast each spell you have access to a number of times equal to your spellcasting ability modifier for that spell, with a minimum of once. This limit applies separately to each named spell, including each cantrip. You regain all expended uses when you finish a Long Rest.

***Multiple Spellcasting Abilities.*** If multiple features allow you to cast the same spell using different abilities, use the highest applicable ability modifier to determine that spell's allowance. All nonexempt castings of that spell share one allowance. Changing the casting source does not restore uses.

***Spell Slots.*** Uses do not replace spell slots. To cast a spell with a slot, you must have both a remaining use and an appropriate slot. Casting the spell with a higher-level slot consumes a use from the same allowance.

***Casting without a Slot.*** A casting that does not expend a spell slot still expends a use, including a ritual or a free casting granted by a feat or other feature, unless an exception applies.

***Counting Castings.*** A casting expends one use even if its attack misses or a target succeeds on its saving throw. Multiple attacks, targets, or subsequent activations produced by a single casting do not expend additional uses. Once you have expended a spell's allowance, you cannot cast it again until you regain a use; attempting an unavailable casting does not expend a slot.

### Warlock Exception

Spells and cantrips acquired through Warlock features are exempt from the casting limit. This includes generic spells selected as Warlock spells. The exemption does not grant additional slots or free castings.

Having levels in Warlock does not exempt spells acquired exclusively through other classes or features. If you acquire the same spell through both Warlock and another source, castings through its Warlock source are exempt; castings through another source follow the shared allowance described above. Using a Pact Magic slot alone does not make a spell acquired exclusively through another source exempt.

## Feats and Other Spell-Granting Features

Being a non-natural spellcaster does not restrict your choice of spell-granting feats, including at character creation. You must meet a feat's normal prerequisites.

You receive all spells, cantrips, free castings, and permissions to use spell slots granted by a feat, species trait, or other feature. Apply that feature's own rules and the campaign casting limit. Generic-list resonance requirements do not apply to this independent access.

## Standard Spellcasting Rules

Normal casting times, components, Concentration, armor training, and other spellcasting requirements apply. 

Material components follow the normal rules. You can use a Component Pouch where the normal component rules permit it.
