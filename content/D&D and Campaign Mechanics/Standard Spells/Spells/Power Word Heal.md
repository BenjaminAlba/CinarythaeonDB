### Power Word Heal

*9th-level enchantment*

**Casting Time:** 1 action

**Range:** 60 feet

**Components:** V

**Duration:** Instantaneous

A wave of healing energy washes over one creature you can see within range. The target regains all its Hit Points. If the creature has the [[Conditions#Charmed|Charmed]], [[Conditions#Frightened|Frightened]], [[Conditions#Paralyzed|Paralyzed]], [[Conditions#Poisoned|Poisoned]], or [[Conditions#Stunned|Stunned]] condition, the condition ends. If the creature has the [[Combat#Being Prone|Prone]] condition, it can use its Reaction to stand up.
