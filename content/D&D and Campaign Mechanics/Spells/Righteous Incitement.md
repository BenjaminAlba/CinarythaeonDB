#### Righteous Incitement
*Evocation Cantrip*
___
- **Resonances:** [[Rhodoklērion]]
- **Casting Time:** Action
- **Range:** 30 feet
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Instantaneous
---
You castigate a creature with words or a derisive strike. A creature you can see within range must make a Wisdom saving throw. On a failed save, the target takes 1d6 Radiant damage, and if it does not target you with an attack before the end of its next turn it takes an additional 1d6 Psychic damage.

***Cantrip Upgrade.*** Both the initial and additional damage increase by 1d6 when you reach levels 5 (2d6), 11 (3d6), and 17 (4d6).
