#### First Speech
*Level 6 Divination*
___
- **Resonances:** [[Mnelyxphora]], [[Rhodoklērion]]
- **Casting Time:** Action
- **Range:** Self
- **Components:** V
- **Duration:** 1 hour
---
When the Tapestry was woven, the first creatures could communicate with one another perfectly through a speech that has long since been lost to time. Though the mortal mind cannot master such speech, you can temporarily learn it using this spell. For the duration you gain the ability to comprehend and verbally communicate with all creatures and plants, and whenever you make a Charisma (Deception, Intimidation, or Persuasion) check, you gain a bonus to the check equal to your spellcasting ability modifier (minimum of +1).
