#### Baffle
*Enchantment Cantrip*
___
- **Resonances:** [[Rhodoklērion]]
- **Casting Time:** Action
- **Range:** 60 feet
- **Components:** V, S, M (an obtusely written book)
- **Duration:** Instantaneous
---
You cause a creature to temporarily forget everything they've ever learned. The target must make an Intelligence saving throw. On a failure, the target takes 1d4 Psychic damage, and the first time it makes a D20 Test before the end of your next turn, it halves all bonuses it would add to that test.

***Cantrip Upgrade.*** The damage increases by 1d4 when you reach levels 5 (2d4), 11 (3d4), and 17 (4d4).
