#### Cascade of Hours
*Level 4 Transmutation*
___
- **Resonances:** [[Qhaevoryth]]
- **Casting Time:** 1 minute
- **Range:** Self
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Instantaneous
---
Time slows to a crawl for you and you alone. You complete one task that you can complete within 8 hours and that doesn't require interacting with another creature, in a pocket outside of time, such as traveling across a city, crafting an item, or reading a book. The stress of being nearly outside the flow of time prevents you from taking a Short or Long Rest while in the temporal pocket.
