#### Warrior's Way
*Level 3 Divination*
___
- **Resonances:** [[Mnelyxphora]], [[Atraphothoromancia]]
- **Casting Time:** Bonus Action
- **Range:** Self
- **Components:** S
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 10 minutes
---
You magically channel the martial wisdom of the multiverse's warriors. For the duration, you can attack twice instead of once whenever you take the Attack action on your turn.

***Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.*** You can attack three times instead of once whenever you take the Attack action on your turn with a level 5+ spell slot.
