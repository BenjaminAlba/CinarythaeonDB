#### Lucky Charm
*Level 5 Transmutation*
___
- **Resonances:** [[Entropomortia]], [[Atraphothoromancia]]
- **Casting Time:** Action
- **Range:** Touch
- **Components:** V, S, M (a four-leaf clover and a shard of broken mirror)
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 hour
---
You touch a Tiny, nonmagical object, infusing it with a portion of your own luck for the duration. While another creature carries the object, that creature has Advantage on D20 Tests, and you have Disadvantage on D20 Tests.

***References***
___
- **Source:** TCM'24, page 130.
- **Classes:** Bard, Paladin, Ranger, Warlock
