#### Caustic Claw
*Transmutation Cantrip*
___
- **Resonances:** [[Atraphothoromancia]], [[Oboromancia]]
- **Casting Time:** Action
- **Range:** Touch
- **Components:** S
- **Duration:** Instantaneous
---
You attempt to strike a creature within range, causing its vital components to turn caustic and self-destructive. Make a melee spell attack against the target. On a hit, you deal 1d4 Acid damage and the target must expend and roll a Hit Point Dice if it has any. The target takes additional Poison damage equal to the result.
***Cantrip Upgrade.*** The Acid damage increases by 1d4 when you reach levels 5 (2d4), 11 (3d4), and 17 (4d4).
