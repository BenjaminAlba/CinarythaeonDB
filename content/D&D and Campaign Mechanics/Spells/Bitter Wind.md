#### Bitter Wind
*Necromancy Cantrip*
___
- **Resonances:** [[Entropomortia]], [[Nihilokathra]]
- **Casting Time:** Action
- **Range:** 30 feet
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Instantaneous
---
The chill of mortality rattles through a creature of your choice within range. The target must succeed on a Constitution saving throw or take 1d8 Cold damage and lose all damage Resistances it has until the start of your next turn, or until it takes damage of a type it normally has Resistance to.

***Cantrip Upgrade.*** The damage increases by 1d8 when you reach levels 5 (2d8), 11 (3d8), and 17 (4d8).
