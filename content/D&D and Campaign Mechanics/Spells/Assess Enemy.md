#### Assess Enemy
*Level 4 Divination*
___
- **Resonances:** [[Entropomortia]]
- **Casting Time:** Bonus action
- **Range:** 60 feet
- **Components:** S
- **Duration:** 1 minute
---
You divine the strengths and weaknesses of a creature you can see within range. You immediately learn if the target is Immune, Resistant, or Vulnerable to any damage types, and if so what types. In addition, for the duration you gain the following benefits:

- You know if the target is Bloodied.
- Your attack rolls against the target benefit from having an additional CRT(1).
- When the target attacks you, you can use your Reaction to give the attack Disadvantage.
