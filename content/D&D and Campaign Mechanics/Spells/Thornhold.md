#### Thornhold
*Abjuration Cantrip*
___
- **Resonances:** [[Viridexaction]]
- **Casting Time:** Action
- **Range:** Self
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** 1 round
---
Phantasmal vines covered in thorns encircle you. You gain 1d4 Temporary Hit Points. If a creature deals damage with a melee attack that causes you to lose Temporary Hit Points gained by this spell, it takes Piercing damage equal to the number of Temporary Hit Points lost. You lose any remaining Temporary Hit Points granted by this spell at the end of your next turn.

***Cantrip Upgrade.*** The Temporary Hit Points granted increase by 1d4 when you reach levels 5 (2d4), 11 (3d4), and 17 (4d4).
