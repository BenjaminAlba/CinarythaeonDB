#### Overexposure
*Divination Cantrip*
___
- **Resonances:** [[Entropomortia]], [[Rhodoklērion]]
- **Casting Time:** Action
- **Range:** 120 feet
- **Components:** V, S, M (a twenty sided die)
- **Duration:** Instantaneous
---
You cause a creature of your choice that you can see within range to shimmer with magical energy. When you do, the target must succeed on a Charisma saving throw or take 1d6 Radiant damage and become overexposed until it takes a Critical Hit or until the start of your next turn. While overexposed, attack rolls against the target have an aditional CRT(1).

***Cantrip Upgrade.*** The damage increases by 1d6 when you reach levels 5 (2d6), 11 (3d6), and 17 (4d6).
