#### Greater Invisibility
*Level 4 Illusion*
___
- **Resonances:** [[Rhodoklērion]]
- **Casting Time:** Action
- **Range:** Touch
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute
---
A creature you touch has the Invisible condition until the spell ends.

___
- **Source:** PHB'24, page 281.
- **Classes:** Bard, Sorcerer, Wizard
