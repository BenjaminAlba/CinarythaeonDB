#### Give Life
*Transmutation Cantrip*
___
- **Resonances:** [[Atraphothoromancia]], [[Viridexaction]]
- **Casting Time:** Action
- **Range:** Touch
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Instantaneous
---
By placing your hand on another creature, you transfer your life force to them. You expend and roll one of your Hit Point Dice. The target regains a number of Hit Points equal to the result of the roll.

***Cantrip Upgrade.*** You can expend and roll an additional Hit Point Die when you reach levels 5 (2 total), 11 (3 total), and 17 (4 total).
