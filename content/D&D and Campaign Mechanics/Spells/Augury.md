#### Augury
*Level 2 Divination*
___
- **Resonances:** [[Entropomortia]], [[Mnelyxphora]]
- **Casting Time:** 1 minute or Ritual
- **Range:** Self
- **Components:** V, S, M (specially marked sticks, bones, cards, or other divinatory tokens worth 25+ GP)
- **Duration:** Instantaneous
---
You receive an omen from an otherworldly entity about the results of a course of action that you plan to take within the next 30 minutes. The DM chooses the omen from the Omens table.

##### Omens
| Omen         | For Results That Will Be... |
|--------------|-----------------------------|
| Weal         | Good                        |
| Woe          | Bad                         |
| Weal and woe | Good and bad                |
| Indifference | Neither good nor bad        |

The spell doesn't account for circumstances, such as other spells, that might change the results.

##### Resonance Variant: Entropomortia or Mnelyxphora

When you cast this spell using Entropomortia or Mnelyxphora as your magical resonance, you may instead contemplate the world's hidden truths, glimpse events of the past or future, or seek an answer to a question that troubles you. The DM determines what you perceive based on the nature of your inquiry and the result of an Arcana check, or an appropriate Charisma or Wisdom check, as determined by the DM.

The greater your success, the clearer and more complete the revelation. On a poor result, the visions may be vague, fragmented, or misleading, and the answer may remain beyond your grasp. The spell does not guarantee that the knowledge revealed is complete or that visions of the future cannot be changed.

If you cast the spell more than once before finishing a Long Rest, there is a cumulative 25 percent chance for each casting after the first that you get no answer.
