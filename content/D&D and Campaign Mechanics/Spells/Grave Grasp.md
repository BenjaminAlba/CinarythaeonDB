#### Grave Grasp
*Level 1 Necromancy*
___
- **Resonances:** [[Nihilokathra]]
- **Casting Time:** Action
- **Range:** 60 feet
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute
---
You cause putrefied hands to burst out of the ground or another surface within range, grasping at a creature of your choice within range. Make a ranged spell attack against the target. On a hit, the target has the Restrained condition. While a creature has the Restrained condition from this spell, it can't regain Hit Points. The target, or another creature within 5 feet of the target, can use its action to free the target, ending the Restrained condition.

***Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.*** You can target one additional creature for each spell slot level above 1.
