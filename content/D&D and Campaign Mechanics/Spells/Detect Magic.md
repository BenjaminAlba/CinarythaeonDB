#### Detect Magic
*Level 1 Divination*
___
- **Resonances:** [[Entropomortia]], [[Rhodoklērion]]
- **Casting Time:** Action (Ritual)
- **Range:** Self (30-foot radius)
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 10 minutes
---
For the duration, you sense the presence of magical effects within 30 feet of yourself. If you sense such effects, you can take the Magic action to see a faint aura around any visible creature or object in the area that bears the magic, and if an effect was created by a spell, you learn the spell's 7.

The spell is blocked by 1 foot of stone, dirt, or wood; 1 inch of metal; or a thin sheet of lead.
