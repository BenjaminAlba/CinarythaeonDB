#### Magecraft
*Divination Cantrip*
___
- **Resonances:** [[Oboromancia]]
- **Casting Time:** Action
- **Range:** Self
- **Components:** V, S, M (a set of Artisan's Tools or a Herbalism Kit)
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute
---
You channel your arcane talent through a set of tools. Choose an *Artisan Tool* or *Herbalism Kit*. Until the spell ends, you add 1d6 to ability checks you make when you take the Utilize action with the chosen tool.

***References***
___
- **Source:** ExE'24
- **Classes:** Artificer, Bard, Wizard
