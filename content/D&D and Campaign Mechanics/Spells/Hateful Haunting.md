#### Hateful Haunting
*Necromancy Cantrip*
___
- **Resonances:** [[Mnelyxphora]], [[Rhodoklērion]]
- **Casting Time:** Action
- **Range:** 60 feet
- **Components:** V
- **Duration:** Instantaneous
---
Choose a creature within range who doesn't have the Deafened condition. The target hears the voices of its deceased ancestors heaping scorn upon it and must succeed on a Charisma saving throw or take 1d4 Psychic damage, plus an additional 1d4 Psychic damage the first time it fails a D20 Test before the end of its next turn.

***Cantrip Upgrade.*** Both the initial and additional damage increase by 1d4 when you reach levels 5 (2d4), 11 (3d4), and 17 (4d4).
