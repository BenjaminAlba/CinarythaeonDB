# Viridexaction
*Nature, vitality, and balance.*

Viridexaction arises from a connection to the material and spiritual worlds. Its magic calls upon a creature's own reserves of vitality to mend wounds and preserve life.

## Resonance: Shared Vitality

While you have resonance with Viridexaction, you gain the following benefit.

***Shared Vitality.*** Once per turn during combat, when you cast a Viridexaction spell, choose yourself or one willing ally you can see within 30 feet of you. You can also choose an Unconscious ally you can see within that range. After the spell's initial effects resolve, the chosen creature can expend one of its unspent Hit Point Dice. If the creature is Unconscious, you can expend one of its unspent Hit Point Dice on its behalf.

Roll the expended die and add the chosen creature's Constitution modifier, for a minimum total of 1. Choose one of the following effects for that creature:

- ***Renewal.*** The creature regains Hit Points equal to the total.
- ***Shelter.*** The creature gains Temporary Hit Points equal to the total. These Temporary Hit Points last until they are depleted or combat ends, whichever comes first.

***Using the Benefit.*** Shared Vitality requires no additional action or Reaction. The die is expended from the chosen creature's pool, not yours, unless you choose yourself. Only one creature can expend one Hit Point Die through this benefit per casting.

The spell needn't restore Hit Points or target the chosen creature. Shared Vitality can activate only when the spell is cast; subsequent effects from that casting, such as healing on later turns, don't activate it again.

An Unconscious creature at 0 Hit Points can regain Hit Points through Renewal, ending its Unconscious condition as normal. Temporary Hit Points from Shelter don't restore it to consciousness.

## Combat Only

All resonance effects function only during combat. You can't use Shared Vitality outside combat, and Temporary Hit Points granted by Shelter end when combat ends.
