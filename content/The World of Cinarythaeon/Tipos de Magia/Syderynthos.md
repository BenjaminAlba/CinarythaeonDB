

## Phonetic Construction:

- _Sider-_ ← from an eroded form of _sidēros_ (iron), but here it no longer strictly means “iron”—it has broadened to imply **all metallic essence and load-bearing matter**
- _-ynth-_ ← synthetic/constructive infix (seen in corrupted technical dialects), implying **artificial formation, imposed structure, or forced cohesion**
- _-os_ ← archaic neutral ending, often used for **systems, doctrines, or naturalized forces rather than locations**

---

## Implied Meaning:

“**The constructed essence of metal**”  
or more obscurely,  
“**That which imposes structure upon the metallic real**”

---

## In-world Meaning / Flavor

Siderynthos is not merely metal manipulation—it is the **authority over cohesion, rigidity, and structural truth**:

- It governs:
    - Metals (iron, steel, alloys, unknown relic materials)
    - Structural integrity (bridges, armor, weapons, frameworks)
    - The _state_ of matter as “held together”
- Practitioners don’t just bend metal—they:
    - **Reinforce** weak structures beyond natural limits
    - **Induce failure** at stress points invisible to others
    - **Redistribute force**, making impacts vanish or transfer elsewhere
    - Temporarily redefine what is “rigid” vs “yielding”

---

## Philosophical Interpretation

Among advanced users, Siderynthos is understood as:

> “All things persist only by agreement of their parts.”

Thus, metal is simply the _clearest expression_ of this principle—not its limit.

---

## Practitioner Title

- **Siderynth** (singular)
- **Siderynthoi** (plural, older form)
- Alternative: **Synthor** (colloquial / militarized term)

---

## Common Misinterpretations (in-world)

- “Iron-bending” (considered reductive, almost insulting)
- “Smith-magic” (used by non-scholars)
- “Weight control” (a partial but flawed understanding)

---

## Whispered Variant Names

As the term spreads and decays across cultures:

- **Serynthos** → simplified, used by common soldiers
- **Therynth** → clipped, used in field commands
- **Siderax** → aggressive, war-cult variant
- **Ynthos** → taboo shortening among those who believe naming the metal is limiting

---

## Forbidden / Advanced Interpretation

A controversial reading suggests Siderynthos is not about metal at all, but:

> “The imposition of structure upon reality itself.”

If true, its highest form would allow:

- Freezing motion mid-action
- Holding collapsing spaces in place
- Preventing _events_ from resolving
