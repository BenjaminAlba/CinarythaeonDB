# Chorothanactis Velarium

**Construction:**

- _Choro-_ (dance, ritual movement)
- _-thanactis_ (fusion of _thanatos_ + _actis_, ray/emanation)
- _Velarium_ (Latin: veil or curtain)

**Lore:**  
Performed with veils and flowing fabrics, this rite “draws the curtain” between planes. The soul is guided as if stepping behind a stage veil, disappearing into unseen reality.

This explains why dark magic also manifests itself from scraps of fabric
