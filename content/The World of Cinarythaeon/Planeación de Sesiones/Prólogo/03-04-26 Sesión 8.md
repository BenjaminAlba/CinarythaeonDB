#### **Elychorion Vathrys**

**Construction:**

- _Elych-_ ← distorted from _helix_ (spiral), via dialect shift
- _-orion_ ← territorial suffix
- _Vathrys_ ← from _bathys_ (deep), mutated into a proper regional name

**Implied Meaning:** “The Deep Spiral Territories”

**Lore:**  
A nation built around descending, spiral cities that tapped into deeper layers of reality. The Tempest sealed the depths. The lower cities still exist—but nothing has come back up.

#### “Ouro‑xenon‑phylakterion” → Final: Orsenaftar
Roots:

ouroboros (self‑devouring)
xenos (foreign, strange)
phylakterion (warding charm)

Morphological evolution:

ouro → or-
xeno → seno → sena
phylakt‑ → ‑aft‑ (internal consonant collapse)
truncated ending ‑erion → ‑ar

Atmosphere:
A world revolving around foreign wards, self‑eating protective magic — ancient sorceries beyond comprehension.
