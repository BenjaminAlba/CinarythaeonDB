8# Escenas (Amanecer de Fuego - Primer día)

## ACT 1 — _Normalcy (1–1.5 hrs)_

### Scene 1: “A Quiet Town”

- Introduce each character in daily life
- Light interactions:
    - Elias with his father
    - Cole studying / talking to Mary
    - Silas being watched / chased
    - Jhonathan with family
    - Gideon arriving

👉 Tone: calm, grounded, human

---

### Scene 2: “The Arrest”

- Elias tracks down Silas
- Instead of arrest → conversation
- Establish moral grayness

👉 Player choice moment (sets tone)

---

### Scene 3: “The Gallows”

- Public execution
- Sheriff Varn presiding
- Condemned man speaks strange words

> Let players feel uncomfortable—not threatened yet

---

## 🌒 ACT 2 — _The Fracture (2–3 hrs)_

### Scene 4: “Something’s Wrong”

- NPCs begin acting off
- Small violence breaks out
- Cole notices patterns

---

### Scene 5: “First Blood”

- Someone snaps—brutal, sudden violence
- Not a monster—just a man

👉 Moral shock moment

---

### Scene 6: “The First Transformation”

- Partial transformation scene
- Someone they know

👉 This is horror, not combat first

---

## 🌕 ACT 3 — _The Hunt (2–3 hrs)_

### Scene 7: “Forming the Group”

- Characters converge
- Gideon pushes for action
- Elias takes leadership

---

### Scene 8: “The Silver Realization”

- Discover silver works
- Improvised weapons

---

### Scene 9: “Personal Losses”

- Each character faces their NPC:
    - Cole → Mary
    - Jhonathan → Eliza
    - Silas → brother

👉 These should HURT

---

## 🌑 ACT 4 — _The Sin (Final 1–2 hrs)_

### Scene 10: “The Source”

- Discover gallows are the anchor
- Ritual explanation

---

### Scene 11: “The Choice”

- Kill infected early?
- Burn bodies?
- Sacrifice someone?

👉 This is where they commit the sin that haunts them

---

### Scene 12: “Ending the Night”

- They “stop” it
- Fire, silver, blood

But:

> It doesn’t feel like victory
