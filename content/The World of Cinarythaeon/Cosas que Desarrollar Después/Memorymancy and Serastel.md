the goddess of memorymancy is still somewehere, died yet defied death, akin to
serastel's story, and to her children, not by birthright but by adoption, the
revelations of the past often feel like kind caresses, even the most horrific of
sights are accompanied by warm embrace, so as to keep them from the harm those
who experienced them lived.

Serastel is drawn by all this, not by will but mere chance

To be a memorymancer is to bandish a curse, at least a social one. Often relegated to live
in pursuit of the powerful or the maquiavelic, memorymancers know better than everyone
to keep some memories to themselves

memorymancy is an impure form of aetheric resonance, it is derived from entropomortia and
ferromancy, and originated in fernokthysyn from the nostalgic longing of their inhabitants, living their tempests through endless, pitch black nights.

"I do not keep them for myself. I still wish to remember, but memories I make of my own"

To their most cherished, the goddess grants them the privilege of oblivion, of not lingering much on the glimpses of past lives.
