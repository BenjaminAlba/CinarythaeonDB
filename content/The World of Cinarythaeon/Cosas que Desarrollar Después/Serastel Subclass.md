
#### Echoes

Allows you to sense the presence of darkness and warp to a nearby ally in combat with darkness

#### Aether Pulsar

Commands all creatures of Darkness within 30ft to make a con saving throw, on a miss, they take 5d8 force damage, on a save, they take 2d8 force damage.

#### Dark Sigil

Places a Dark Sigil on an enemy (Nakarkos' Eye), casting their spirits onto an endless battle with their brander.
